## Summary & Key Takeaways

- Anthropic has merged its Claude Cowork and chat functionalities.
- The goal is to create a single, unified Claude experience.
- This aims to simplify interaction and reduce user confusion.
- The change is rolling out to Pro and Max plans first.

## Our Commentary

This is a smart move from Anthropic. I've personally found the distinction between Cowork and regular Claude a bit fuzzy. Consolidation makes sense, especially as these models become more agentic. It feels like a natural evolution.
