## Summary & Key Takeaways

- Hyperscript component callbacks now receive every argument passed to them.
- This includes arguments handled by default or rest parameters.
- The change ensures more consistent and predictable argument forwarding.

## Our Commentary

Another small but useful refinement for Solid's hyperscript. It's about making the developer experience smoother and more consistent, especially for those using more advanced function signatures in their components.
