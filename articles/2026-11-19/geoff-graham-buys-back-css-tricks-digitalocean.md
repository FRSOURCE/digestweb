## Summary & Key Takeaways

- Geoff Graham has reacquired CSS-Tricks from DigitalOcean.
- He plans to transform CSS-Tricks into a community-driven, inclusive learning platform.
- The news has been met with widespread encouragement from the web development community.
- This move signals a potential resurgence for independent web blogging and education.

## Our Commentary

Oh, this is just _chef's kiss_. I genuinely didn't see this coming, and it's such a breath of fresh air. We've all watched so many beloved web resources get swallowed up and then slowly fade. The idea of CSS-Tricks returning to its roots, under Geoff's guidance, feels like a win for everyone who believes in independent content. I'm rooting for him, and selfishly, for us all.
