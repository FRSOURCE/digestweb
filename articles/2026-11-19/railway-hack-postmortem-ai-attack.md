## Summary & Key Takeaways

- Cloud platform Railway published a detailed postmortem of a recent hack.
- The attackers likely utilized AI models that lacked security safeguards.
- Railway has since hardened its systems in response to the incident.
- The event provides crucial lessons on securing platforms against AI-assisted attacks.

## Our Commentary

Another AI-related security incident. This is becoming a pattern, isn't it? The idea of "no-safeguards AI models" being weaponized is a terrifying thought. It's not just about data breaches anymore; it's about the tools used to _facilitate_ those breaches. We need to be thinking about AI security from every angle, not just how our own models are protected, but how they might be used against us.
