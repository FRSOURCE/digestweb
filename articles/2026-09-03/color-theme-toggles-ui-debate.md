## Our Commentary

This is a classic UX debate, and it's great to see it resurface with fresh perspectives. The 'system preference' option often feels like the most user-friendly, but there are valid arguments for simpler toggles. It reminds us that even seemingly small UI decisions can spark significant discussion among experts. I lean towards three states, personally.
