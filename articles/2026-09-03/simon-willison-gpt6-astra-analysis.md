## Summary & Key Takeaways

- GPT-6 Astra is rolling out to users and APIs, priced competitively with Claude Fable 5/5.1.
- It shows impressive scores on ARC-AGI 3 (99.9% with custom harness) and security benchmarks like ExploitBench (100%).
- Astra demonstrates strong long-context capabilities, achieving high accuracy up to 1M tokens.
- Despite its strengths, it trails Claude Fable 5.1 and Meta's Muse Spark 1.3 on Artificial Analysis's Intelligence Index.
- It leads the Coding Agent Index for cost efficiency, indicating strong performance in code-related tasks.
