## Summary & Key Takeaways

- WebKit has opened the call for proposals for Interop 2027.
- Interop is an annual initiative focused on improving web platform compatibility.
- The goal is to reduce fragmentation and ensure consistent behavior across different browsers.
- Community input is essential for identifying key areas for standardization and improvement.

## Our Commentary

Interop is one of the most important initiatives for the health of the web platform. This call for ideas is a chance for everyone to influence what gets prioritized for cross-browser consistency. We encourage everyone with a pain point to speak up. It's how we make the web better for everyone.
