## Summary & Key Takeaways

- Playco used GPT-6 Astra to rapidly prototype three distinct game themes.
- All prototypes were built from a single grey box foundation.
- The use of Astra resulted in a 50% reduction in manual fixes.
- This demonstrates Astra's capability in accelerating creative and iterative development processes.
