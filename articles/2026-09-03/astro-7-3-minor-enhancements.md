## Summary & Key Takeaways

- Adds an `--ignore-lock` flag for `astro preview` to bypass lockfile checks.
- Passes Astro's runtime logger to custom image services and cache providers.
- Ships a `finalize()` helper for Cloudflare custom worker entrypoints.

## Our Commentary

A minor release from Astro, but we appreciate the continued refinement. The `--ignore-lock` flag is a small quality-of-life improvement. It's good to see better integration with Cloudflare workers, too. These small updates add up over time.
