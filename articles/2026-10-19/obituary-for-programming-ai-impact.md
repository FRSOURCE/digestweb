## Summary & Key Takeaways

- Simon Willison reflects on the changing nature of programming due to AI coding agents.
- He quotes Jeremy Scheff on the loss for programmers who enjoyed solving 'tricky little logic puzzles.'
- The article contrasts the experience of puzzle-solving programmers with those who focus on building systems.
- It explores the real impact of LLMs on the enjoyment and definition of a programming career.
- The piece serves as a commentary on the evolving developer experience in the AI era.

## Our Commentary

Simon Willison's 'Obituary for Programming' really hit me. The idea that AI is taking away the 'tricky little logic puzzles' that made programming so enjoyable for many of us is a tough pill to swallow. I've always loved the problem-solving aspect, and if agents are just churning out the solutions, what's left? It's a half-formed thought, but there's something unsettling about the potential loss of that intellectual challenge. We're gaining efficiency, but at what cost to the craft?
