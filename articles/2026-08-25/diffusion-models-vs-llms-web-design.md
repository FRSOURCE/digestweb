## Summary & Key Takeaways

- The article discusses using AI for prompt-to-website design.

- Diffusion models are suggested to be more creative and visually detailed.

## Our Commentary

This is a fascinating question. I've seen some incredible visual output from diffusion models, and the idea of applying that directly to web design, focusing on the visual first, makes a lot of sense. LLMs are great for code, but design is inherently visual. I'm genuinely curious if this approach will yield more innovative and aesthetically pleasing results. It feels like a natural fit.
