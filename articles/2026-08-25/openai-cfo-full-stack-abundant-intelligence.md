## Summary & Key Takeaways

- OpenAI's CFO outlines the "full stack" enabling abundant intelligence.
- Advances in chips, compute, models, and products are key drivers.
- These factors compound to deliver more useful AI.
- The goal is greater scale and lower cost for AI solutions.

## Our Commentary

It's interesting to hear the CFO's perspective on the AI stack. We often focus on the models, but the underlying infrastructure — chips, compute — is just as critical for scaling. "Abundant intelligence" is a bold vision, and it makes me wonder about the societal implications of such widespread, cheap AI. It's a lot to unpack.
