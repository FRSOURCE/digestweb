## Summary & Key Takeaways

- OpenAI has unveiled its custom inference chip, named Jalapeño.
- First results indicate industry-leading speed and efficiency.
- The chip aims for faster and more power-efficient AI inference.
- It promises higher throughput and lower latency for modern models.
