## Summary & Key Takeaways

- The article argues websites should continuously evolve post-launch.
- It introduces "autonomous websites" optimized by AI agents.
- This approach aims to combat website stagnation due to lack of maintenance time.
- Pierre Burgy shares insights from building for full website autonomy.

## Our Commentary

"Autonomous websites, continuously optimized by agents after launch." This is both exciting and a little terrifying. On one hand, the idea of a website that never stagnates is incredibly appealing. On the other, the thought of agents churning away at 3 AM, making changes without human oversight... I genuinely don't know how to feel about this. It's a huge shift in how we think about web development and maintenance.
