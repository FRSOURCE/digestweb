## Summary & Key Takeaways

• The article introduces a shape-aware ASCII renderer implemented using Three.js.
• It moves beyond conventional luminance-based ASCII art generation.
• Each character cell dynamically searches 95 glyphs to best represent the underlying shape.
• The demo showcases the Codrops mark as a draggable 3D solid rendered in ASCII on the GPU.
• This technique offers a more detailed and visually rich ASCII representation.
