## Summary & Key Takeaways

- Anthropic has extended access to Claude Fable 5 for paid plans until July 19.
- This extension is attributed to ongoing compute constraints.
- Users can utilize up to half their weekly limit on Fable 5, then switch models or use credits.
- OpenAI's GPT-5.6 Sol appears to have unrestricted access, creating a competitive disadvantage for Anthropic.
- Simon Willison advocates for Anthropic to make Fable permanently available to retain users.

## Our Commentary

Another Fable bump. This back-and-forth with Anthropic's access policies is starting to feel a bit like a game of chicken. I get the compute constraints, I really do, but the uncertainty is a killer for developers trying to build on these platforms. OpenAI's confidence with GPT-5.6 Sol access just makes Anthropic's position look shakier. We need stability, not these constant extensions. It makes me wonder if they're truly struggling with capacity or if it's a strategic play that's backfiring.
