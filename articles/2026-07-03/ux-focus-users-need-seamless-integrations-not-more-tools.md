## Summary & Key Takeaways

The article posits that users are overwhelmed by too many standalone tools.
The real need is for seamless integrations of useful features.
Integrations should align with users' already established mental models.
This approach aims to enhance daily workflows rather than disrupt them.
The piece is brought to you by a video course on UX and design patterns.

## Our Commentary

I mean, yes, obviously. This feels like a fundamental UX principle that's been around forever. It's not wrong, but it's not exactly groundbreaking either. The promotional aspect for the course makes it feel a bit less like a pure thought piece. We've been saying "less friction, more flow" for decades.
