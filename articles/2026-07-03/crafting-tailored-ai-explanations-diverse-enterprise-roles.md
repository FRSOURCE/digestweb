## Summary & Key Takeaways

Different roles within an enterprise require distinct types of AI explanations.
Explanations should be tailored to the specific context and needs of the user.
This customization helps improve understanding and trust in AI systems.
A one-size-fits-all approach to AI explanations is ineffective.
Consider the user's technical background and decision-making responsibilities.

## Our Commentary

This is a good point, and one we often overlook when building AI systems. It's not enough to just _have_ an explanation; it needs to be _understandable_ and _relevant_ to the person receiving it. The challenge is designing those dynamic explanations without adding too much cognitive load to the system itself. It's a tricky balance.
