## Summary & Key Takeaways

- Native PyTorch backends for vLLM and SGLang inference engines are now available on TPU.
- These engines enable running and optimizing serving workloads using familiar PyTorch tools.
- Optimized TPU kernel implementations and production-ready features like KV cache management are included.
- Disaggregated serving is supported for enhanced flexibility and scalability.
- Public benchmarks demonstrate state-of-the-art performance and industry-leading cost efficiency on Ironwood TPU.
