## Summary & Key Takeaways

- PyTorch 2.15 includes enhanced support for Metal Performance Shaders (MPS).
- This update aims to improve the performance of PyTorch models on Apple hardware.
- MPS leverages Apple's integrated GPUs for accelerated machine learning workloads.
- The improvement benefits developers working with PyTorch on macOS and iOS devices.

PyTorch 2.15 getting better MPS support is a solid, if niche, improvement.
