## Summary & Key Takeaways

- Anthropic tasked an internal research Claude model with designing protein minibinders.
- The goal was to create small proteins that could tightly attach to scorpion neurotoxins.
- This research demonstrates Claude's capabilities in complex scientific design tasks.
- The project showcases a practical application of LLMs in biotechnology and medicine.
