## Summary & Key Takeaways

- Simon Willison developed 'Battle Drones,' a browser-based RTS game, with significant help from AI coding agents.
- The game features 3D models, fog of war, multiple biomes, and a random map generator.
- It includes skirmish and last stand modes with various units and area effect weapons.
- A standout feature is multiplayer support for nearby devices using audio to synchronize game state.
- The project demonstrates the power of AI in accelerating complex game development.

## Our Commentary

Simon Willison's 'Battle Drones' is just pure joy. An RTS game, built with coding agents, and _audio-based multiplayer_?
