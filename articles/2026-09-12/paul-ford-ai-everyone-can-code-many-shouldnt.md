## Summary & Key Takeaways

- Paul Ford suggests that AI makes it easy to write software, but also to do it badly.
- He argues that truly cutting-edge software still requires human thought and collaboration.
- The quote implies that while AI democratizes coding, it also clarifies the value of skilled human developers.
- The piece touches on the paradox of increased accessibility versus quality in AI-assisted development.

## Our Commentary

This quote from Paul Ford really resonates with us. It's a sharp observation on the current state of AI in development. I've seen firsthand how AI can accelerate simple tasks, but also how it can obscure fundamental issues if not used thoughtfully. The idea that 'everyone can code, but many shouldn’t' feels like a necessary counterpoint to the hype. It reminds us that critical thinking and craft remain irreplaceable.
