## Summary & Key Takeaways

Perplexity now uses OpenAI's GPT-6 Astra for core business functions.
Astra handles tasks like writing communications and modifying software.
It also monitors production systems autonomously.
Human intervention is significantly reduced compared to older models.
This demonstrates a high level of trust in advanced AI for critical operations.
The integration suggests a new era of AI-driven operational autonomy.
