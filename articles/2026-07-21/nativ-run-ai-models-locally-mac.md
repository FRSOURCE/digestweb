## Summary & Key Takeaways

- Nativ is a new macOS app for running AI models locally.
- Developed by Prince Canuma, creator of MLX-VLM.
- It provides a user-friendly chat interface and a localhost API server.
- The app integrates with existing Hugging Face MLX model caches.

## Our Commentary

Local AI is where it's at for privacy and control. Nativ looks like a fantastic step forward for Mac users, making it genuinely easy to experiment with models without cloud dependencies. Simon Willison's endorsement is a strong signal. I'm excited to try this out.
