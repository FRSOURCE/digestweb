## Summary & Key Takeaways

- Using fewer subagents can improve AI agent quality, cost, and latency.
- Progressively disclosed context and tools are preferred over numerous subagents.
- The article provides guidance on when and how to migrate subagents into the main loop.
- This optimization strategy is relevant for agents built on the Claude Platform.

## Our Commentary

This is the kind of practical advice we need as agentic AI becomes more prevalent. It's easy to over-engineer with subagents, and seeing a clear strategy for optimization is helpful. I appreciate the focus on cost and latency; those are real-world constraints that often get overlooked in the hype. It's a good reminder that simpler architectures can often be more effective.
