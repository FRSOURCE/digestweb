## Summary & Key Takeaways

- Anthropic donated its Agent Skills standard to the Agentic AI Foundation (AAIF).
- The AAIF, co-founded by Anthropic, Block, and OpenAI, is under the Linux Foundation.
- This move aims to ensure Agent Skills remain open, community-driven, and vendor-neutral.
- It follows Anthropic's previous donation of the Model Context Protocol (MCP) to the AAIF.
- Simon Willison had predicted this move back in December.

## Our Commentary

This is a smart move by Anthropic. Open standards are absolutely crucial for the healthy evolution of the agentic AI ecosystem. I'm glad to see the AAIF becoming a central hub for these foundational specifications. Simon Willison's prediction coming true just adds a nice touch to the story. It feels like the industry is slowly, but surely, moving towards more interoperable and less walled-garden AI.
