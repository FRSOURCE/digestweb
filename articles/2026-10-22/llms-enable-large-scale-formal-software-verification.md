## Summary & Key Takeaways

- Formal verification, traditionally limited to small codebases, is becoming scalable with AI models.
- Anthropic used Claude to prove security properties in over one hundred open-source libraries.
- The process was completed over a span of a few weeks, demonstrating efficiency.
- This approach could significantly enhance software security by identifying bugs at a larger scale.

## Our Commentary

This is genuinely exciting. Formal verification has always felt like the holy grail of software security, but the manual effort was prohibitive. If LLMs can genuinely automate this at scale, it's a game-changer. I'm curious about the false positive rates and the depth of properties they can prove, but the potential here is immense. It's a tangible application of AI that could make our software stacks much safer.
