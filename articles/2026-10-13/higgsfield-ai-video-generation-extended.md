## Summary & Key Takeaways

- Higgsfield now generates AI videos over 10 minutes in length, up from 1 minute.
- It leverages GPT-6 Astra for longer video generation.
- GPT-Image-2.5 provides precise visuals for the generated content.
- The platform is paving the way for voice filmmaking with GPT-Live.

## Our Commentary

Ten minutes of AI-generated video? That's a huge leap. We've been seeing these short, impressive clips, but extending the duration significantly changes the game for what's possible.
