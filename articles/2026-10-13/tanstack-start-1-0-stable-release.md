## Summary & Key Takeaways

- TanStack Start has achieved its 1.0 stable release.
- It is now considered ready for production use.
- This marks a significant milestone for the TanStack ecosystem's entry into meta-frameworks.

## Our Commentary

A 1.0 stable release from TanStack is always a big deal. We've seen the quality of their libraries like Query and Router, so a full meta-framework from them is exciting. I'm curious to see how it carves out its niche in an already crowded space. The TanStack philosophy of framework-agnostic utilities is strong, so I expect Start to bring that same level of thoughtful design to the full-stack experience.
