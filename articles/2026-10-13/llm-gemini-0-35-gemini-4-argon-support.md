## Summary & Key Takeaways

- `llm-gemini` 0.35 adds support for the new Gemini 4 Argon model.
- Gemini 4 Argon is available via the Gemini API and a $100/month subscription.
- Initial pricing is $2/million input and $10/million output, doubling on December 1st.
- The article provides benchmark cost comparisons, showing Argon as competitive.
- Gemini models are noted for producing excellent SVGs, including pelican illustrations.

## Our Commentary

Gemini 4 Argon is here, and Simon Willison is on it with `llm-gemini` support. The pricing details are always the most interesting part for me. That price doubling on December 1st is a bit of a curveball, making it align with Opus 5.5. It really drives home that raw token cost isn't the only metric; what matters is how efficiently a model achieves a goal within an agentic workflow. The benchmark comparisons are super valuable for anyone trying to pick the right model.
