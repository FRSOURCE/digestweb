## Summary & Key Takeaways

- `web-edit` 0.1a1 has been released.
- Performance optimizations were written by Claude Opus 5.5.
- Claude's work was based on a Python 3.15 profiling report.
- The profiling report itself was generated by GPT-6 Astra.
- This showcases a fully AI-assisted optimization workflow.

## Our Commentary
