## Summary & Key Takeaways

• The article discusses the evolution of web interfaces beyond traditional buttons and forms.
• It highlights a shift towards experiences driven by human intent, powered by AI.
• UX designers are challenged to adapt their roles from visible interfaces to transparent AI guidance.
• The concept of "no interface" suggests a future where interactions are more natural and intuitive.
• This paradigm shift has significant implications for the future of web development and design.

## Our Commentary

"The Death of the Button" – what a title! This article hits on something we've been feeling for a while. As AI gets smarter, the need for explicit UI elements diminishes. It's a fascinating, slightly unsettling thought for us as web developers. Are we designing for humans or for the AI that interprets human intent? It's a fundamental shift in how we think about interaction, and I'm not sure we're fully ready for it.
