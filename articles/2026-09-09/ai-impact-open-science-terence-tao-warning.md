## Summary & Key Takeaways

- AI is rapidly solving open scientific problems, potentially depleting the pool of fruitful research questions.
- The speed of AI-powered effort can "flatten" problems before human researchers fully explore them.
- This creates an incentive for researchers to withhold promising directions, reversing open science traditions.
- Such a shift could cause serious long-term damage to the future of scientific fields.

## Our Commentary

This is heavy. I'm genuinely unsettled by the idea of AI consuming the very wellspring of human curiosity. We've always relied on the slow burn of shared problems. The thought of that being strip-mined for quick solutions feels... wrong. It's like we're accelerating towards a future where the joy of discovery is outsourced, and the process itself becomes a race against machines. What happens when all the "easy" problems are gone?
