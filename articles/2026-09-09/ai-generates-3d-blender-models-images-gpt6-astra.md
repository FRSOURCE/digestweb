## Summary & Key Takeaways

- The article showcases AI's ability to generate 3D Blender models from images.
- GPT-6 Astra and Codex were used to create the models.
- An image of a Fabergé egg was used as the prompt input.
- The AI successfully generated multiple `.blend` files.
- A new `.blend` URL viewer tool was created to display these models in the browser.
- This highlights AI's growing capabilities in creative 3D content generation.
