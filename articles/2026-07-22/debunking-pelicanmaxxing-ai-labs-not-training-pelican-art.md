## Summary & Key Takeaways

- Dylan Castillo conducted a deep-dive study into the "pelicanmaxxing" theory.
- The theory suggested AI labs were secretly optimizing models to draw pelicans on bicycles.
- He tested 7 different models with 48 unique animal-vehicle prompts.
- The results showed no evidence that pelicans or bicycles were drawn better than other subjects.
- No lab demonstrated a specific advantage in generating pelicans on bicycles.
- The study concluded that pelican-bicycle scenes do not appear memorized or specially trained.
