## Summary & Key Takeaways

- Qwen 3.8 27B achieved a score of 52 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index.
- This score matches GPT-5.6 Luna (max) and is just one point behind GLM-5.2 (753B) and DeepSeek V4 Pro 0813 (1.6B).
- The Qwen model has significantly fewer parameters (27B) compared to its high-performing peers.
- This performance suggests remarkable efficiency and capability for a model of its size.
