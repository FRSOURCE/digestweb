## Summary & Key Takeaways

- Traditional chat interfaces obscure the progress of AI agents, making complex tasks hard to follow.
- Canvases provide a visual, structured environment to track and manage agentic workflows.
- This approach improves the steerability of agents, allowing for better intervention and guidance.
- Visualizing workflows helps in identifying inefficiencies and optimizing resource usage.
- The article advocates for integrating canvases into agent development for clearer oversight.

## Our Commentary

We've all been there, watching an AI agent churn through tasks, wondering what it's actually doing. The idea of using canvases to visualize these workflows feels like a breath of fresh air.
