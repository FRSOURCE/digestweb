## Summary & Key Takeaways

- LLMs are eroding trust in software documentation and the organizations producing it.
- Signals previously used to mark mature systems are now easier to counterfeit.
- Engineers may approach new software with increased suspicion.
- Maintainers face a challenge in ensuring and demonstrating software and documentation quality.

## Our Commentary

This quote from Kyle Kingsbury hits hard. I've felt this creeping unease myself. When you can't trust the docs, what _can_ you trust? It's not just about accuracy; it's about the very foundation of knowledge transfer in our field. We're entering a weird era where the source of truth is constantly in question. It's a messy problem, and I don't see an easy fix.
