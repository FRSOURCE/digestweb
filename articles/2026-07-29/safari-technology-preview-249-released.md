## Summary & Key Takeaways

- Safari Technology Preview 249 is now available for download.
- This release includes the latest updates and improvements from WebKit.
- It targets macOS Golden Gate and macOS Tahoe users.
- Developers can use it to test upcoming web platform features and bug fixes.

## Our Commentary

Another Safari Technology Preview release. These are always good to keep an eye on for early glimpses of what's coming to the web platform. It's a steady drumbeat of progress, even if individual releases are often incremental.
