## Summary & Key Takeaways

- Cypress Cloud introduces re-run optimization for CI pipelines.
- The system now skips tests that passed in the current build.
- This significantly reduces re-execution time and CI minutes used.
- It also lowers billable test results for Cypress Cloud users.
- The feature works out-of-the-box with common CI providers.
- Customization options are available for specific needs.
- This optimization is available for Business plans and higher.
- Free trials can also access this functionality.

## Our Commentary

This is a smart move from Cypress. We've all been there, waiting for a full suite to re-run just because one flaky test failed. Saving CI minutes and getting to green faster is a tangible win. I wonder how robust the 'current build' detection is, but the premise is solid. It's a clear value-add for their paid tiers.
