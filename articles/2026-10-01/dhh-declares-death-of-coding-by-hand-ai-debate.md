## Summary & Key Takeaways

- DHH, creator of Ruby on Rails, made a bold statement at Rails World.
- He declared the end of writing code by hand for professional work at 37signals.
- This statement has ignited a new debate about AI's role in coding.
- The article questions if this shift is an unstoppable trend.
- It explores the implications for software developers and the industry.
- The discussion centers on the future of human-computer collaboration in coding.

## Our Commentary

DHH always knows how to stir the pot, doesn't he? 'Death of coding by hand' is a strong claim, and it's definitely going to get people talking. We've been seeing AI assist more and more, but a complete replacement? That's a leap. I think it's more about augmentation and shifting skill sets, but the debate itself is crucial for understanding where we're headed.
