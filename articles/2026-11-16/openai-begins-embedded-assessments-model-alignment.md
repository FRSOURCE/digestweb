## Summary & Key Takeaways

- OpenAI launched its initial phase of embedded assessments.
- External partners like Apollo Research and METR are conducting independent evaluations.
- The assessments focus on model alignment, safeguards, and monitoring systems.
- This initiative aims to enhance trust and transparency in AI development.

## Our Commentary

This is a good step towards transparency, I guess? "Embedded assessments" sounds a bit like letting the fox guard the hen house, even with external partners. I'm always a little skeptical of self-regulation in tech, but independent evaluation is better than none. We need more clarity on the _results_ of these assessments, not just that they're happening.
