## Our Commentary

AI agents in healthcare? This is a double-edged sword. On one hand, the potential for efficiency is massive. On the other, the stakes are incredibly high. I'm thinking about the ethical implications, the data privacy, the potential for misdiagnosis. We need to be _extremely_ cautious and ensure robust human oversight. This isn't just about productivity; it's about patient lives.
