## Summary & Key Takeaways

- The article offers an overview of sourcemaps and their function.
- Sourcemaps are crucial for debugging processed JavaScript and CSS.
- They map compiled code back to its original source files.
- Understanding sourcemaps improves the developer experience in complex build environments.

## Our Commentary

Sourcemaps are one of those unsung heroes of web development. We all use them, but how many of us really understand how they work? It's easy to take them for granted until they break. A good refresher like this is always welcome, especially for newer devs who might not grasp the magic happening behind the scenes.
