## Summary & Key Takeaways

- Daybreak Red is now accessible in AWS GovCloud (US-East).
- It features the GPT-5.6 Cyber model.
- Availability is for approved customers only.
- Access is provided through Amazon Bedrock.
- The service is intended for authorized cybersecurity work.
