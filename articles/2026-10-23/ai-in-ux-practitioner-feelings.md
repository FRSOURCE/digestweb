## Summary & Key Takeaways

- AI tools are making UX practitioners' work faster.
- Despite speed gains, most practitioners have lukewarm feelings about AI.
- Mandates to use AI are a source of frustration.
- Unreliable AI output remains a significant concern.
- Inflated expectations for AI's capabilities often diminish perceived value.

## Our Commentary

This NNGroup report on AI in UX is exactly what we need: a dose of reality. It's not just about the tech; it's about the human experience of using it. "Mixed feelings" and "unreliable output" are sentiments I hear constantly. We're still figuring out how to integrate AI effectively without adding more friction than it removes.
