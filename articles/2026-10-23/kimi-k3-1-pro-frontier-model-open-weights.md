## Summary & Key Takeaways

- Moonshot has released Kimi K3.1 Pro, a new frontier AI model.
- It shows benchmark performance comparable to GPT-6 Astra and Claude Opus 5.5.
- Full model weights are slated for release by November 4, 2026.
- Pricing is competitive, matching GPT-6.1 Sol.
- This is the first time a soon-to-be open-weight model has scored so highly against frontier models.
