## Summary & Key Takeaways

- Simon Willison experimented with Claude Opus 5.5.
- The AI generated a tool that paints photos onto a canvas element.
- It uses simulated paintbrush strokes and Sobel edge detection.
- No external libraries were used beyond Google Fonts.
- This builds on previous experiments with AI-simulated art.
