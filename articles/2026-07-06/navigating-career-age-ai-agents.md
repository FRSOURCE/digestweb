## Summary & Key Takeaways

- AI excels at tasks with clear, definable answer keys.
- Human careers will increasingly focus on areas without predetermined solutions.
- The article encourages focusing on creativity and problem-solving beyond automation.
- It prompts reflection on the unique value humans bring in an AI-driven world.

## Our Commentary

Addy always makes me think. This isn't just about AI taking jobs; it's about what _kind_ of work remains uniquely human. I find myself wondering if "no answer key" is truly the differentiator, or if AI will just generate its own answer keys. It's a bit unsettling, honestly, but a necessary conversation.
