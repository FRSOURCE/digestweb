## Our Commentary

Connecting LLMs to sensitive data like health records is a huge step, but also a massive responsibility. The 'securely access' part is doing a lot of heavy lifting here. While the potential benefits for clinicians are clear, I can't help but feel a knot in my stomach about the privacy implications. We've seen how these systems can hallucinate; in healthcare, that's not just inconvenient, it's dangerous.
