## Summary & Key Takeaways

- Hugging Face has released `@huggingface/kernels`, a library of 200+ WebGPU kernels.
- These kernels enable high-performance local AI inference directly in web browsers.
- This significantly reduces reliance on server-side processing for AI tasks.
- It opens up new possibilities for privacy-preserving and offline AI applications on the web.
- The initiative leverages WebGPU for GPU-accelerated computations in the browser.
