## Summary & Key Takeaways

Simon Willison developed a tool to identify clichés in LLM-generated text.
The tool was created out of frustration with repetitive AI writing styles.
It highlights ten common patterns found in such content.
The app helps users spot "no fluff, no filler, no jargon" type phrases.
This utility offers a way to critique and improve AI-assisted writing.
