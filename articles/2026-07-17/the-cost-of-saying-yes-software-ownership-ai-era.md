## Summary & Key Takeaways

AI has significantly reduced the cost of writing new code.
The cost of owning and maintaining code, however, remains high.
This creates a new challenge for engineering teams in the AI era.
A framework is introduced to help teams decide which changes are truly "cheap."
The framework considers factors beyond initial development, like maintenance and future adaptability.

## Our Commentary

This is a conversation we need to have. I've seen so much "AI makes coding faster!" talk, but the ownership part is always glossed over. GitHub bringing this up feels like a necessary dose of reality. We're all about shipping, but what about the debt we're accumulating? It's a good gut check.
