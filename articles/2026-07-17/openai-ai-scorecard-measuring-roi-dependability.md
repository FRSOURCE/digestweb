## Summary & Key Takeaways

OpenAI introduces a new AI scorecard for practical measurement.
The scorecard aims to quantify the return on investment (ROI) of AI initiatives.
Key metrics include useful work completed by AI systems.
It also tracks the cost associated with each successful AI task.
Dependability of AI outputs is a core component of the evaluation.
The framework considers the return generated per unit of compute resources.

## Our Commentary
