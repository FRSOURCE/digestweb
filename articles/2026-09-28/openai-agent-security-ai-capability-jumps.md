## Summary & Key Takeaways

- Joe Daroo, OpenAI's Agent Security lead, discusses the surprising speed of AI capability jumps.
- He emphasizes the difficulty these rapid advancements create for organizational security.
- The commentary highlights the need for resilient people, systems, and processes.
- Organizations must prepare for unexpected AI capability surges.
- It stresses the importance of robust incident response and communication strategies.

## Our Commentary

This quote from Joe Daroo is chillingly honest. The idea that even the people building these systems are "surprised at the jump and suddenness" of capabilities is unsettling. It makes me question how much control anyone truly has. We need to be asking ourselves, as developers, how we build systems that can adapt to such unpredictable technological shifts.
