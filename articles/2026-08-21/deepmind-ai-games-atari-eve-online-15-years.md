## Summary & Key Takeaways

- Google DeepMind celebrates 15 years of AI research applied to gaming.
- The journey began with mastering classic Atari games.
- Current efforts involve prototyping advanced AI gameplay in complex environments like EVE Online.
- Partnerships with game studios aim to push the boundaries of AI in interactive entertainment.

## Our Commentary

DeepMind's trajectory in games is always fascinating. Moving from Atari to EVE Online is a massive leap in complexity. I'm curious how much of this translates to general-purpose AI agents versus highly specialized game-playing AI. It's a good showcase of what's possible.
