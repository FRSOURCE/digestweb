## Summary & Key Takeaways

- The article advocates for the critical importance of dark mode.
- It argues that dark mode is more than just a visual preference.
- The author highlights dark mode's significant accessibility benefits.
- It can reduce eye strain and improve readability for many users.
- The piece encourages developers to prioritize dark mode implementation.
- It suggests that neglecting dark mode can exclude certain user groups.

## Our Commentary

Dark mode. Yes. Absolutely. I'm with them on this one. It's not just about looking cool; for some of us, it's genuinely about reducing eye strain and making the web usable for longer periods. I get that it's extra work for designers and developers, but I think we've moved past the point where it's a "nice-to-have." It feels like a fundamental accessibility feature now. I wish more sites would treat it as such from the start.
