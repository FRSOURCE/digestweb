## Summary & Key Takeaways

- Andrej Karpathy built "HackerTube," a YouTube clone, in two hours.
- He used an advanced AI agent, Opus 5.5, for the entire process.
- Opus 5.5 collected and filtered 6,454 YouTube videos recommended on Hacker News.
- The agent handled UI creation, deployment, security, and optimization.
- HackerTube offers quality content, custom recommendations, and keyboard shortcuts.
- The project demonstrates the rapid development capabilities of AI agents.
- It provides an alternative to mainstream YouTube's recommendation algorithms.
