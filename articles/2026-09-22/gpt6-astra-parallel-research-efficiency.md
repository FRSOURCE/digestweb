## Summary & Key Takeaways

- GPT-6 Astra enabled Parallel's agents to significantly improve research efficiency.
- The model helped reduce the time required for labor-market data research by half.
- Operational costs for these research tasks were also cut by 50%.
- This demonstrates Astra's capability in accelerating data synthesis and analysis.
- The improvements were observed when compared against prior AI models used by Parallel.
