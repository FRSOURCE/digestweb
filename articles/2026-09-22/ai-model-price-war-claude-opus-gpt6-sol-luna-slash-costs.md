## Summary & Key Takeaways

Anthropic released Claude Opus 5.5.
OpenAI launched GPT-6 Sol and GPT-6 Luna.
GPT-6 Sol and Luna are priced at half their GPT-5.6 equivalents.
GPT-6 Luna is now one of OpenAI's cheapest models ever at $0.10/$0.50 per million tokens.
The new pricing significantly undercuts competitors like Grok 4.7.
GPT-5.6 models face a scheduled 25% price increase, making GPT-6 even more competitive.
Visual comparisons of model outputs show distinct stylistic differences between the 5.6 and 6 families.

## Our Commentary

Okay, this is wild. We just got hit with a barrage of new models and a price war that feels like it came out of nowhere. GPT-6 Luna at half the price? That's a game-changer for developers building on these platforms. I'm genuinely curious how long this aggressive pricing can last, and what it means for smaller players in the LLM space. It's a race to the bottom, but for now, our wallets are winning.
