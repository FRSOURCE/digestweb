## Summary & Key Takeaways

- Bidirectional text (Bidi) involves mixing left-to-right and right-to-left languages within a single sentence.
- Properly rendering Bidi text is crucial for internationalization and accessibility on the web.
- The article argues that developers often misunderstand or overlook Bidi complexities.
- It highlights a significant limitation: large language models (LLMs) like ChatGPT do not inherently understand Bidi.
- Developers must understand Bidi principles to implement it correctly, as AI tools won't solve it for them.

## Our Commentary

Bidi text is one of those things that sounds simple until you actually have to implement it. This article is a good reminder that not everything can be offloaded to an LLM, especially when it comes to nuanced UI/UX and accessibility concerns. It's a niche topic, but a really important one for building truly global web experiences. We appreciate the call to human understanding over AI reliance here.
