## Summary & Key Takeaways

- Extreme space weather can damage power systems and degrade GPS.
- Microsoft Research developed a new machine learning system.
- This system forecasts where damage is likely to occur.
- Predictions are made 30-60 minutes before a storm's arrival.
- The goal is to mitigate risks to critical infrastructure.
