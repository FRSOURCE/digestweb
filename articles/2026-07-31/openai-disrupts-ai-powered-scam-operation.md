## Summary & Key Takeaways

- OpenAI identified and disrupted a Cambodia-based criminal scam operation.
- The operation leveraged ChatGPT to support various illicit activities.
- Scams included investment fraud, romance scams, gambling schemes, and impersonation.
- This action demonstrates OpenAI's commitment to combating malicious AI use.

## Our Commentary

It's good to see concrete actions against the misuse of AI. The potential for LLMs to scale up scam operations is genuinely concerning, and reports like this show that the platforms are actively fighting back. It's a constant cat-and-mouse game, but every disruption helps. We need more transparency on these efforts.
