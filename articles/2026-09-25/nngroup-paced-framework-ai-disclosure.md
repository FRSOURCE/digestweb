## Summary & Key Takeaways

- Introduces the PACED Framework for AI disclosure.
- Provides guidance on when to inform users about AI use.
- Considers audience, context, and degree of AI involvement.
- Aims to address ethical considerations and user trust in AI.

## Our Commentary

The PACED Framework sounds like a much-needed tool. The question of "when to disclose AI" is becoming increasingly complex. It's not a simple yes/no, and having a structured approach from a UX authority is incredibly valuable. We need to build trust, not just features; this is a step in the right direction.
