## Summary & Key Takeaways

- Companies are facing a "Tokenpocalypse" due to high AI consumption costs.
- An Accenture anecdote reveals non-engineers are major drivers of token usage.
- Converting PDFs to images and then to markdown is cited as a "big token chewer."
- The article highlights the need for better understanding and management of AI resource usage.
- It suggests that PDFs are a terrible medium for communicating information efficiently with AI.

## Our Commentary

"Tokenpocalypse" – we love it. This is such a real problem that many organizations are just starting to grasp. It's not just about the raw compute; it's about the sheer inefficiency of how people interact with these models. The PDF example is painfully relatable. We've seen similar patterns, and it's a stark reminder that user education and thoughtful integration are just as important as the models themselves.
