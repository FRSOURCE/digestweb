## Summary & Key Takeaways

- The article presents the PROVE framework for evaluating AI tools.
- It emphasizes that adoption pressure doesn't equate to utility.
- The framework tests one AI tool against a single, defined task.
- It aims to produce a defensible, provisional decision on an AI tool's worth.
- This helps cut through AI hype to find practical value.

## Our Commentary

We're seeing so much AI hype right now, and it's easy to get swept up in it. This PROVE framework from NNGroup is exactly what we need: a grounded, practical approach to evaluating whether an AI tool actually solves a problem or just adds complexity. It's a good reminder that not every shiny new thing is a silver bullet. We appreciate the focus on defensible decisions over blind adoption.
