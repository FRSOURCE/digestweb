## Summary & Key Takeaways

- The article introduces the concept of UX-Context Design.
- It discusses the shift in UX research output from human-readable documents to AI-guiding context.
- The focus is on using UX knowledge to inform and shape AI-generated interfaces.
- This represents a new paradigm in the design process driven by AI integration.

## Our Commentary

This is the kind of article that makes me pause and think about the future of our craft. The idea that UX designers will be "curating context" for AI rather than just designing for humans is a profound shift. We're going to need new skill sets, new ways of thinking. I'm not sure if I'm excited or terrified, but it's definitely happening.
