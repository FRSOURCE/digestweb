This is a critical piece. LLM chat interfaces are popping up everywhere, and if we don't bake in accessibility from the start, we're just creating new barriers. Eric Bailey's insights are always spot-on, and this article feels incredibly timely. We need to be thinking about this now, not as an afterthought. It's not just about compliance; it's about fundamental human access.
