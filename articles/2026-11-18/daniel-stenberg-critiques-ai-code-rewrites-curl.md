## Summary & Key Takeaways

- Daniel Stenberg, curl's creator, expresses skepticism about AI code rewrites.
- He notes that AI agents converted 185,000 lines of C to 340,000 lines of Rust.
- During the same period, human developers merged over 300 commits and 200 bug fixes in curl.
- Stenberg questions if AI clones can keep up with human-driven improvements and vulnerability fixes.
- The piece underscores the ongoing value of human expertise in critical open-source projects.

## Our Commentary

This is the kind of grounded reality check we desperately need. Daniel Stenberg's perspective on AI code rewrites for `curl` is absolutely spot on. It's easy to get swept up in the "AI will write all the code" narrative, but the messy, continuous work of fixing bugs and patching vulnerabilities? That's where human expertise still shines. We're not just writing code; we're maintaining trust.
