## Summary & Key Takeaways

- OpenAI is measuring the impact of coding agents on accelerating AI research.
- Performance of these agents remains uneven across different tasks.
- The research explores what this unevenness signals about recursive self-improvement in AI.
- Understanding these dynamics is crucial for future AI development strategies.

## Our Commentary

The idea of AI agents accelerating AI research is both fascinating and a little terrifying. "Jagged frontier" is a perfect description; it's not a smooth ascent. We're seeing these tools push boundaries, but the inconsistencies are a stark reminder that human oversight is still critical. The implications for recursive self-improvement are huge.
