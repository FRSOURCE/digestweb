## Summary & Key Takeaways

- Nik Suresh's perspective highlights the overwhelming AI mania in large companies.
- Executives are crafting AI strategies for multi-billion dollar organizations without ever using AI tools themselves.
- Engineers are resorting to rewriting entire codebases in different languages to meet internal "token leaderboards."
- Vendor executives are pressured to validate absurd 100x productivity claims from customers to avoid contract cancellations.
- This dynamic creates an environment where honesty about AI's true capabilities is suppressed.
- The article suggests a systemic issue where fear of undermining customer credibility drives irrational decisions.
