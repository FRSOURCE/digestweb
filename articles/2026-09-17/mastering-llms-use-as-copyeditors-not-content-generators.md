## Summary & Key Takeaways

- Thomas Ptacek suggests a strict rule: never use a single word an LLM suggests for writing.
- LLMs are best utilized for fact-checking, spelling, grammar, and as a thesaurus.
- The "weird smell" of LLM-generated text is a good indicator to avoid it.
- Adopting this principle helps maintain writing discipline.
- The article provides insights into building a personal LLM copyediting tool.

## Our Commentary

"You may not use a single word an LLM suggests to you." I love this rule. It's so simple, so absolute, and it immediately resonates with that "weird smell" we all recognize in AI-generated text.
