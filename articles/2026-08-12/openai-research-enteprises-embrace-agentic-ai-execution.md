## Summary & Key Takeaways

- OpenAI's research explores enterprise AI adoption trends.
- Focuses on the shift from AI assistance to agentic AI for execution.
- Highlights the use of ChatGPT and Codex in enterprise settings.
- Identifies "frontier firms" leading the charge in AI integration.

## Our Commentary

This is a big deal. The shift from "assistance" to "execution" with agentic AI is where things get really interesting—and potentially scary. We're moving past copilots to autonomous agents. I'm always a bit wary of corporate case studies, but OpenAI's own research on this trend is worth paying attention to.
