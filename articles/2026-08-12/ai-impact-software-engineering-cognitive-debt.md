## Summary & Key Takeaways

- Florian Herrengt's quote highlights a growing concern in AI-assisted development.
- The piece suggests AI might be contributing to increased cognitive debt in software projects.
- Developers may lose understanding of complex systems built with AI-generated code.
- The scenario describes AI failing to fix bugs and developers relying on AI for basic system knowledge.
- This raises questions about the long-term implications for software engineering roles.

## Our Commentary

This quote from Florian Herrengt hits hard. The idea of projects becoming so convoluted that "no one on your team could possibly start to understand what's going on" because AI built it, and AI can't even fix it, is genuinely unsettling. We're already seeing hints of this. I worry about the erosion of fundamental understanding and the potential for opaque, unmaintainable systems. It's a future I don't want to build.
