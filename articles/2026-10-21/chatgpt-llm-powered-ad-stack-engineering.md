## Summary & Key Takeaways

- OpenAI built ChatGPT's ad stack using LLMs.
- The system is designed to understand conversational intent for ad targeting.
- A core principle is keeping ads distinct and separate from AI-generated answers.
- The goal is to deliver relevant advertisements at scale.
- The article provides insights into the technical implementation for user trust and relevance.

## Our Commentary

An LLM-powered ad stack is certainly a novel approach. The focus on understanding conversational intent for relevance is smart, but the "keeping ads separate from answers" part is where the rubber meets the road for user trust. I'm always wary of ads creeping into AI interactions, so the engineering choices here are critical. It's a complex problem to solve well.
