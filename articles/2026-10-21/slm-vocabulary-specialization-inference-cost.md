## Summary & Key Takeaways

- Specializing the vocabulary of Small Language Models (SLMs) is a new optimization technique.
- This method can reduce inference costs by up to 77%.
- The cost reduction is observed in real-world applications.
- This research has significant implications for making SLMs more efficient and accessible.
- The findings were shared on the Hugging Face blog.
