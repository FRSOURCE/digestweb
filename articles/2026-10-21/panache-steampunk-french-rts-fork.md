## Summary & Key Takeaways

- "Panache" is a French-language fork of the open-source mobile RTS game "Battle Drones."
- The game has been reskinned to a Belle Époque (1900s France) steampunk theme.
- It features new animated intros, steampunk models, and French-inspired music.
- The fork primarily focuses on aesthetic updates rather than gameplay changes.
- This project exemplifies the creative potential of open-source collaboration.

## Our Commentary

This is just pure joy. Seeing an open-source game get such a creative and charming reskin is exactly what I love about the community.
