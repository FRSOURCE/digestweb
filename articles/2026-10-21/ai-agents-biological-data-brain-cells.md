## Summary & Key Takeaways

- OpenAI collaborated with PerturbAI and Scripps Research.
- They deployed thousands of distributed AI agents.
- The agents analyzed data from millions of brain cells.
- The goal was to uncover new and promising research leads in biological data.
- This showcases a powerful application of AI in scientific discovery.

## Our Commentary
