## Summary & Key Takeaways

- Andy Clarke's new e-book, "Standout Designs for the Web," is now available.
- The book encourages designers to move past generic templates.
- It advocates for creating websites with distinct personality.
- Clarke emphasizes unique layouts over conventional approaches.
- The interview provides insights into the book's core philosophy.

## Our Commentary

I appreciate the push for more personality in web design. It feels like we've been stuck in a rut of template-driven sameness for a while now. It's a good reminder that design isn't just about functionality; it's about expression too. We need more of this kind of thinking.
