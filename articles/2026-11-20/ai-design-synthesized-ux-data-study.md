## Summary & Key Takeaways

- AI-generated designs are improved by providing synthesized data.
- Raw user comments alone lead to less effective AI designs.
- The study highlights the importance of human synthesis in the AI design pipeline.
- Designers should process raw data into findings and recommendations for AI.
- This optimizes AI's ability to create better design solutions.

## Our Commentary

This is a really practical insight. It confirms what many of us suspected: AI isn't a magic bullet that can just ingest anything and spit out gold. It still needs human intelligence to refine the input. It's a good reminder that the "AI" in "AI design" still benefits immensely from the "human" in "human-centered design." We're not out of a job yet, folks.
