## Summary & Key Takeaways

- AI platforms are experiencing widespread "token fraud" through free trial abuse.
- Users exploit free access for unauthorized AI usage, token resale, and commercial ventures.
- One platform, ChatPRD, observed unusual prompts like "pelicans riding bicycles" used to identify underlying models.
- This abuse is forcing AI companies to eliminate free trials and re-evaluate access strategies.

## Our Commentary

This is genuinely unsettling. We've talked about the potential for AI misuse, but this kind of systematic token fraud feels like a new level of parasitic behavior. It's a stark reminder that every new technology brings new vectors for abuse. I feel for the developers trying to build useful tools, only to have their resources drained by bad actors. It makes me wonder how sustainable any "free tier" for AI will be in the long run.
