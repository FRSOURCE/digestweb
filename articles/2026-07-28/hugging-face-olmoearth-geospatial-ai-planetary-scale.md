## Our Commentary

Planetary-scale geospatial inference? That's a mouthful, but the implications are massive. Think climate modeling, urban planning, disaster response – all powered by AI. We're moving beyond just text and images; AI is now directly interacting with and interpreting our physical world at an unprecedented scale. This is a big step.
