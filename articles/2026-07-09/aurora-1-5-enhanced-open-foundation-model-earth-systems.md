## Summary & Key Takeaways

- Aurora 1.5 is an update to an open foundation model for weather and Earth systems.
- It introduces 22 additional variables for more comprehensive data analysis.
- The model now supports hourly temporal resolution.
- Probabilistic ensemble forecasting has been added to improve prediction accuracy.
- These enhancements make it more useful for real-world climate and energy applications.
