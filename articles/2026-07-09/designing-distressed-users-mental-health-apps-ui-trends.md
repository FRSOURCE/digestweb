## Summary & Key Takeaways

- UI trends often conflict with the specific needs of mental health applications.
- Mental health apps require designs that reduce cognitive strain and build trust.
- The article introduces an evaluation framework for assessing UI patterns.
- It emphasizes creating a sense of refuge for distressed users.
- Designers are encouraged to prioritize user well-being over fleeting UI fashions.
