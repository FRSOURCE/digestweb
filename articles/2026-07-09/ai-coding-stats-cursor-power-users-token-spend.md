## Summary & Key Takeaways

- Power users of AI coding tools generate ten times more lines of code than median users.
- The majority of AI expenditure comes from input tokens rather than output tokens.
- Almost 50% of AI-generated code changes are accepted by developers without manual review.
- These statistics offer insights into developer productivity and trust in AI tools.

## Our Commentary

These stats from Cursor are genuinely eye-opening. 10x code generation for power users? And nearly half of AI changes accepted without review? That's a level of trust and efficiency I didn't expect so soon. It makes me wonder about the quality of the code being generated and the potential for subtle bugs to slip through. We're definitely in a new era of development.
