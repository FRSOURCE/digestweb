## Summary & Key Takeaways

• Replit has launched a new "Free Mode" for its software creation platform.
• This mode is powered by OpenAI's latest model, GPT-5.6 Luna.
• It allows users to develop software without incurring token costs.
• The initiative aims to make AI-assisted coding more accessible to a wider audience.

## Our Commentary

A free tier for AI-powered coding? This is huge for accessibility. We've seen so many barriers to entry with AI tools, especially around cost.
