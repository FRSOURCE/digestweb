## Summary & Key Takeaways

- The post quotes Bruce Schneier on the purpose of writing assignments.
- Schneier views writing as "gym tasks" for developing critical thinking.
- He argues that constant mental exercise is vital to prevent skill atrophy.
- The piece implicitly questions AI's role in potentially undermining these developmental tasks.

## Our Commentary

Schneier always cuts right to the core. The "gym tasks, not work tasks" analogy for writing is brilliant, and it perfectly frames my own anxieties about AI. If we outsource all the thinking, what happens to our ability to think? It's a question we all need to grapple with, and I genuinely don't know how to feel about the long-term implications.
