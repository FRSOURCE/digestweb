## Summary & Key Takeaways

- OpenAI announced a massive 80% price drop for GPT-5.6 Luna and 20% for GPT-5.6 Terra.
- The price reduction was enabled by GPT-5.6 Sol optimizing the model's forward pass and kernel code.
- GPT-5.6 Sol autonomously rewrote and optimized production kernels using Triton and Gluon.
- GPT-5.6 Luna is now significantly cheaper than competitors like Gemini 3.1 Flash-Lite and Claude Haiku 4.5.
- This move drastically changes the landscape for lower-priced, high-performance models.
