## Summary & Key Takeaways

- Battledrones v14 introduces a new four-player map named "Compass Rose."
- Multiplayer mode now supports up to four human players via WebSockets.
- AI (Claude Opus 5.5) assisted in generating the new map and modifying network code.
- New robot team colors, purple and green, join the existing red and blue.
- AI players fill any unused slots in four-player matches.

## Our Commentary
