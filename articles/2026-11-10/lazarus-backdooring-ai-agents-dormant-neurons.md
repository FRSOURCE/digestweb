## Summary & Key Takeaways

- Microsoft Research identified "Lazarus," a new AI backdoor technique.
- It involves reprogramming dormant neurons within an AI model.
- A hidden trigger can activate these reprogrammed neurons to execute malicious commands.
- The threat is particularly severe for AI agents with tool or terminal access.
