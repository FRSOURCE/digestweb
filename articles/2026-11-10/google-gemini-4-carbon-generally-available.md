## Summary & Key Takeaways

- Google's Gemini 4 Carbon model is now publicly available.
- It was previously restricted to a subset of cyber defenders.
- The model is priced at $2/million input and $10/million output at launch.
- Prices are set to double to $4/$20 on December 1st.
- Simon Willison demonstrates its capabilities, including 3D animation generation.

## Our Commentary

Gemini 4 Carbon is finally out for everyone! I've been waiting for this. The pricing is aggressive at launch, but that December 1st price hike is a bit of a gut punch. Still, seeing it generate a Three.js animation of a mech stomping around NYC is wild. The potential for creative coding with these models is just exploding. I'm excited to play with this.
