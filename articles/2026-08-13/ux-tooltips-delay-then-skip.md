## Summary & Key Takeaways

- The article discusses optimal UX for tooltips.
- It advocates for a slight initial delay to prevent accidental triggers.
- For nearby or related tooltips, the delay should then be skipped for fluidity.
- This approach aims to balance discoverability with avoiding annoyance.
- The piece emphasizes thoughtful design for common UI elements.
