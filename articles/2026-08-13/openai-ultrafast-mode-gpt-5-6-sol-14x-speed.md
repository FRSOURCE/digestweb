## Summary & Key Takeaways

- OpenAI is previewing "Ultrafast," a new API service tier.
- This mode boosts GPT-5.6 Sol's speed by up to 14 times.
- It achieves an output rate of up to 750 tokens per second.
- The performance gains are powered by Cerebras hardware.
- This significantly enhances the real-time capabilities of GPT-5.6 Sol.
