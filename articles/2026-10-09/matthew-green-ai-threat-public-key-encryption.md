## Summary & Key Takeaways

- Matthew Green expresses concern about AI's rapid progress versus slow human standards updates.
- He estimates a 15% chance of losing confidence in existing public-key encryption.
- There's a 1% chance of entering "Minicrypt," where public-key encryption is impossible.
- Proactive preparation is essential to mitigate these AI-driven security risks.

## Our Commentary

Matthew Green's warning is chilling. The idea that AI could break fundamental cryptography faster than we can adapt is a terrifying thought. We're so reliant on these systems. It's a stark reminder that the "move fast and break things" mentality doesn't apply to foundational security. This isn't just a technical problem; it's a societal one.
