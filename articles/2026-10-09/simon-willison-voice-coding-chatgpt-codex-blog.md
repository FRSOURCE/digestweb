## Summary & Key Takeaways

- Simon Willison developed a new blog feature using voice commands.
- He utilized the ChatGPT desktop app's Codex tab with voice conversation mode.
- The process involved creating Django models, views, templates, and import functions.
- This showcases a practical application of AI for hands-free coding.
- The experience highlights the potential for voice-driven developer workflows.
