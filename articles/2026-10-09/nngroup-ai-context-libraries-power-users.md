## Summary & Key Takeaways

- AI power users frequently struggle with managing their context files.
- Many users find it difficult to locate their existing context libraries.
- The effectiveness of an AI agent is directly tied to its context library.
- This points to a significant usability challenge in current AI tools.

## Our Commentary

This resonates so much. We're all trying to figure out how to "prompt engineer" or "context engineer" our way to better AI results, but if the tools themselves make managing that context a nightmare, we're hitting a wall. It's a clear signal that AI interfaces need to evolve rapidly to support these new workflows.
