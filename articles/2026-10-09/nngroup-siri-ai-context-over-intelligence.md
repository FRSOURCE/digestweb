## Summary & Key Takeaways

- A useful AI assistant relies heavily on relevant personal context.
- The ability to effectively use this context is a key strength.
- User experience should help clarify the AI's limitations.
- Raw intelligence alone is not sufficient for a truly helpful AI assistant.

## Our Commentary

This is a really important distinction for AI product design. We get so caught up in "how smart" an AI is, but NNG reminds us that _utility_ often comes down to context and managing expectations. It's a good framework for thinking about how to build AI that people actually want to use, not just marvel at.
