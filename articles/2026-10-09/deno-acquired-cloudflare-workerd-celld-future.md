## Summary & Key Takeaways

- Cloudflare has acquired the Deno runtime and its team.
- Cloudflare will cease active development on the Deno runtime within a year.
- The focus will shift to `workerd` and `celld`, Cloudflare's open-source runtime and Durable Objects implementation.
- Deno creator Ryan Dahl cites Deno's "gravity well of Node compatibility" as a reason for the shift.
- This move aims to build new server development abstractions, moving beyond Node.js reimplementation.
- Deno will remain open source for community maintenance.

## Our Commentary

Wow. Just... wow. Deno joining Cloudflare is big, but the news that Cloudflare will _stop_ maintaining the Deno runtime after a year is a gut punch. Ryan Dahl's explanation about the "gravity well of Node compatibility" is brutally honest, and I get it. It's a tough spot to be in, trying to innovate while constantly being pulled back to Node.js parity. This feels like a definitive end to the "Node.js killer" narrative, and a clear pivot towards Cloudflare's `workerd` vision. I'm genuinely sad for the Deno community, but also intrigued by what `celld` might become.
