## Our Commentary

I find myself nodding along with Nathan Lambert here. The hype cycle around AGI is intense, and it's easy to get swept up. It's refreshing to hear a researcher articulate a more grounded view: rapid progress, yes, but maybe not the sci-fi leap we sometimes imagine. It's a good dose of reality.
