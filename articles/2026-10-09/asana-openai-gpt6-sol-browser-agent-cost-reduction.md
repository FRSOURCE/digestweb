## Summary & Key Takeaways

- Asana utilized OpenAI's GPT-6 Astra model in Codex for its browser agent.
- This integration resulted in a 76x reduction in model costs during tests.
- The browser agent also saw a 5x improvement in speed.
- The advancements enable Asana to provide more capable AI models to its users.
- GPT-6.1 Sol is highlighted as a key component in these efficiency gains.
