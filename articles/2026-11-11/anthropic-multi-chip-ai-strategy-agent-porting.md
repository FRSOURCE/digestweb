## Summary & Key Takeaways

- Anthropic's IPO roadshow video revealed details about their business and technical approach.
- They employ a multi-chip strategy, training and serving Claude across AWS, Google, NVIDIA, and AMD GPUs.
- This approach requires tailored software and inference stacks for each chip family.
- An AI agent named Mythos successfully ported Claude to AMD GPUs in three days.
- Anthropic focuses on "compute multipliers" to increase total effective compute.
- These multipliers allow for training more intelligent models with the same physical compute.
