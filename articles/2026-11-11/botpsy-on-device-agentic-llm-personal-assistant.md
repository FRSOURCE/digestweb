## Summary & Key Takeaways

- BotPsy is an on-device personal assistant utilizing an agentic language model.
- The model operates with a sub-billion-parameter count, emphasizing efficiency.
- This research focuses on bringing advanced AI capabilities to edge devices.

## Our Commentary

On-device AI is where it's at for privacy and speed. A sub-billion-parameter agentic model for personal assistants? That's genuinely exciting. We're seeing a real push to make these powerful models run locally, which I think is a huge win for users. It feels like a step towards truly personal, private AI.
