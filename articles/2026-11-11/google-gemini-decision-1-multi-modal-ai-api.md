## Summary & Key Takeaways

- Google has launched "Gemini Decision 1," a new AI decision model API.
- It directly competes with Jev and OpenAI's decision models.
- Gemini Decision 1 is fully multi-modal, handling text, images, audio, video, and PDFs.
- The API supports yes/no, choice, and rubric-scoring questions.
- Pricing is competitive, positioned between Jev and OpenAI's Luna model.
- Simon Willison tested its video analysis capabilities with promising results.
