## Summary & Key Takeaways

- TanStack AI provides strongly typed libraries for integrating AI features into applications.
- The introduction covers fundamental setup and prompting techniques.
- It demonstrates how to handle streaming responses from AI models.
- Guidance is provided on persisting AI conversation threads to a database.
- The article also explains how to resume an AI response after a mid-stream refresh.

## Our Commentary

TanStack is a name we trust, so seeing them jump into the AI space with a strongly typed library for app integration is genuinely exciting. This feels like a practical, developer-first approach to bringing AI into our apps, rather than just another wrapper. I'm particularly interested in the persistence and streaming aspects; those are often the trickiest parts to get right.
