## Summary & Key Takeaways

- The Claude Code team uses AI agents to manage high-volume code reviews.
- Claude agents perform initial self-verification of their generated code.
- A risk-tiered human approval process follows the AI's initial review.
- The article provides a checklist for implementing similar AI-assisted review practices.

## Our Commentary

This is exactly the kind of practical AI integration we've been waiting for. The idea of agents reviewing their own work before human eyes even touch it is both exciting and a little unsettling. We're moving into a world where the "human in the loop" might be more of a final gatekeeper than an active participant in every step. I wonder about the edge cases and how much trust is truly placed in the AI's self-correction.
