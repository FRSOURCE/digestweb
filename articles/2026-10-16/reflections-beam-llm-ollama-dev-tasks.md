## Summary & Key Takeaways

- Reflection has released its first open-weight model named Beam.
- Beam is specifically engineered for software engineering and reasoning tasks.
- It also supports long-running agentic tasks.
- The model is currently available for free on Ollama for a limited time.
- Users can access Beam via Ollama Cloud.

## Our Commentary

Another open-weight model hitting the scene, and this one is specifically tuned for software engineering and agentic tasks. That's a big deal for developers looking to integrate more sophisticated AI into their workflows. The "limited time free" aspect is a classic move, but it's a great opportunity to kick the tires on something potentially powerful. We're seeing a clear trend towards specialized LLMs for dev.
