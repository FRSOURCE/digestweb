## Summary & Key Takeaways

- Mistral has released a preview of Mistral Large 4.
- It is a 1 trillion parameter model, with 49 billion active parameters.
- The model was trained on 3,800 NVIDIA Grace Blackwell GPUs.
- Open weights are promised for release by the end of the month.
- Mistral Large 4 shows significant performance improvements over its predecessor.
- It supports "none" and "high" reasoning levels via the API.
