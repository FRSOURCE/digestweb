## Summary & Key Takeaways

- Simon Willison tasked Claude Opus 5.5 with composing computer game music.
- The LLM also designed a text-based format for the music and example tracks.
- The generated music was surprisingly competent, reminiscent of "Monkey Island."
- This experiment suggests a potential new emergent capability for text models in music composition.
- Further research is needed to confirm if this is a recent development in LLM abilities.

## Our Commentary

This is genuinely cool. An LLM composing music, and doing it _well_? That's a capability I hadn't fully anticipated. It makes me wonder if we're seeing another emergent property, similar to how 3D graphics generation improved. The idea of "vibe-coding" music with an AI is both exciting and a little bit terrifying.
