## Summary & Key Takeaways

The article discusses Sophie Alpert's policy on acceptable AI writing use by engineers.
Engineers must fully stand behind every idea and sentence in their AI-assisted documentation.
It is unacceptable to blame AI for unclear or incorrect statements.
Any rewrite or rephrase by an LLM changes the original meaning.
Information is lost when an AI, lacking full human context, transforms text.
The author stresses the importance of human oversight and responsibility in AI-generated content.

## Our Commentary

This hits home. The idea that there are "no lossless transformations" when AI rewrites text is something we all need to internalize. I've felt that subtle shift in meaning myself when using LLMs for drafting. Simon's point about engineers owning every word, even AI-generated ones, is spot on. It's not just about accuracy; it's about intent and nuance. This isn't just a policy; it's a fundamental truth about language and AI.
