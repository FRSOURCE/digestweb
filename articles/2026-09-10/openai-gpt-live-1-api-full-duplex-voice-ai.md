## Summary & Key Takeaways

- OpenAI has released GPT-Live-1 in its API.
- This new model enables natural, full-duplex voice conversations.
- It offers stronger instruction following capabilities.
- Developers can now implement custom voices.
- The API includes direct telephony support.
- This significantly enhances the realism and interactivity of voice AI experiences.
