## Summary & Key Takeaways

- Shopify is transitioning its mobile development from React Native to native Swift and Kotlin.
- The company previously adopted React Native in 2020 for cross-platform efficiency.
- The primary reason for the shift is the emergence of AI agents.
- AI agents can now handle significant implementation, translation, testing, and review tasks.
- This reduces the overhead of maintaining separate native codebases.
- Shopify will rehome or archive its significant React Native open-source libraries.

## Our Commentary

Shopify moving _away_ from React Native, and explicitly citing AI agents as the reason? This is a bombshell. We've been debating cross-platform vs. native for years, and now AI is throwing a wrench into the whole discussion. It makes me wonder if the "build once, deploy everywhere" dream is being reshaped by AI's ability to bridge the native gap. This is a huge signal.
