## Summary & Key Takeaways

- The article investigates how LLMs manage their reasoning processes.
- It explores the concept of "reasoning effort" within LLMs.
- LLMs can learn to operate in low-, medium-, and high-effort reasoning modes.
- Understanding these modes is crucial for developing more efficient and capable AI.
- The research aims to provide insights into the internal workings of LLM cognition.
