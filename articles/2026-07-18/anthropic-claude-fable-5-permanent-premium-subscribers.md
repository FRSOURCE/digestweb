## Summary & Key Takeaways

- Claude Fable 5 will be permanently included in Max and Team Premium plans.
- Access will be at 50% of the usual usage limits for these plans.
- Pro and Team Standard users retain access via usage credits and receive a $100 credit.
- This decision reverses Anthropic's previous plan to remove Fable 5 from subscriptions.
- Market competition from models like GPT-5.6 Sol is cited as a likely factor.
- The change addresses user concerns about Fable 5's future availability.
- Users on the $20/month plan will still not have access to Fable 5.

## Our Commentary

Well, this is a relief for many, I imagine. Anthropic's initial plan to pull Fable 5 from subscriptions felt like a misstep, especially with the competition heating up. It's a clear sign that the LLM market is fiercely competitive, and companies are having to adapt quickly. I'm glad they listened to user feedback, even if it means they had to re-evaluate their compute strategy.
