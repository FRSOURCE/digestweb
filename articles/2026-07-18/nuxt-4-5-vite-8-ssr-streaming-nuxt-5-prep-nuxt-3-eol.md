## Summary & Key Takeaways

- Nuxt 4.5.0 is a minor release with major internal upgrades.
- It now runs on Vite 8 and Rspack 2, bringing performance improvements.
- An experimental SSR streaming mode is introduced for enhanced server-side rendering.
- The release includes new composables and conventions for developers.
- Significant groundwork has been laid to prepare for the upcoming Nuxt 5.
- Nuxt 3 will reach its end-of-life on July 31, 2026.
- Users are encouraged to migrate from Nuxt 3 to Nuxt 4 soon.
- A compatibility option `future.compatibilityVersion: 5` is available for testing Nuxt 5 breaking changes.

## Our Commentary

This is a chunky release, and we're seeing Nuxt really push forward. Vite 8 and Rspack 2 are big under-the-hood wins for performance. The experimental SSR streaming mode is intriguing; I'm always looking for ways to squeeze more out of server rendering. The Nuxt 3 EOL isn't a surprise, but it's a firm deadline for those still clinging on. It feels like the team is doing a lot of heavy lifting to make the Nuxt 5 transition as painless as possible, which I appreciate.
