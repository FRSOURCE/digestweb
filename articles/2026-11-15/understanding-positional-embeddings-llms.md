## Summary & Key Takeaways

- The article explains the fundamental concept of positional embeddings in LLMs.
- It details how these embeddings allow models to understand the order of tokens in a sequence.
- Various methods for representing token positions are explored.
- Understanding positional embeddings is key to grasping LLM architecture and function.

## Our Commentary

Positional embeddings are one of those core LLM concepts that can feel a bit like magic if you don't dig into them. This kind of deep-dive is exactly what we need to demystify how these models actually work. I appreciate the focus on foundational knowledge rather than just reporting on the latest model.
