## Summary & Key Takeaways

- Tech companies are increasingly building internal platforms for non-engineers.
- These platforms enable the creation of internal websites and tools.
- Ramp and Stripe are cited as examples of companies adopting this trend.
- The trend is described as fostering 'vibe-coding apps' within organizations.
- This approach aims to empower more employees to contribute to internal tooling.

## Our Commentary

The 'vibe-coding apps' trend is fascinating. It's essentially low-code/no-code for internal tools, but with a more... human touch? I'm always interested in how companies empower non-engineers to build. It speaks to a broader shift in developer experience, where the goal is to make creation accessible to everyone. It's a good sign for internal DX, but I also wonder about the maintenance burden down the line.
