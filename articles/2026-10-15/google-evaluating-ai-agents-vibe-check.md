## Summary & Key Takeaways

- The guide offers a practical engineering approach to AI agent evaluation.
- It provides a reference architecture for continuous evaluation pipelines.
- The focus is on multi-turn AI agents, a complex area of interaction.
- It leverages Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform, Google ADK, and OpenInference standards.
- The goal is to move beyond subjective assessments to objective, continuous evaluation.

## Our Commentary

Moving past the 'vibe check' for AI agents? Yes, please. This is a crucial topic. Evaluating complex, multi-turn AI agents is incredibly difficult, and Google providing a practical engineering guide and reference architecture is a huge step forward. I've seen too many projects get stuck because they couldn't reliably measure agent performance. This is the kind of foundational work that truly enables robust AI development.
