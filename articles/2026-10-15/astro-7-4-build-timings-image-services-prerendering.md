## Summary & Key Takeaways

- The new `--timings` flag helps developers analyze build performance by showing time spent on different tasks.
- Separate build and runtime image services offer more granular control over image optimization.
- A `notFound()` handler is now available for custom entrypoints, improving error handling.
- Experimental parallel prerendering aims to speed up static site generation.
- Custom redirect pages provide more flexibility for routing.

## Our Commentary

Astro continues its steady march forward. I'm always a fan of anything that helps me understand where my build time is going, so that `--timings` flag is a welcome addition. The experimental parallel prerendering also sounds like a solid step towards even faster builds. It's these kinds of iterative improvements that make a framework feel truly robust.
