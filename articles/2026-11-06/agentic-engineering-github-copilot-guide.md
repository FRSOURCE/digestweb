## Summary & Key Takeaways

- GitHub Copilot is evolving beyond just code generation.
- The concept of 'agentic engineering' is introduced, where AI assists in broader development tasks.
- Copilot can help with planning, writing code, reviewing changes, and investigating production issues.
- The article guides developers on integrating AI agents into their workflow.
- Human judgment remains crucial even with advanced AI assistance.

## Our Commentary

This 'agentic engineering' idea is fascinating, and a little unsettling. We're moving from AI as a helper to AI as a collaborator, or even an orchestrator. I wonder how much human oversight will truly be needed when agents are churning away at 3 AM. It feels like a step towards a future where the developer's role shifts dramatically, and I'm not sure we're all ready for it.
