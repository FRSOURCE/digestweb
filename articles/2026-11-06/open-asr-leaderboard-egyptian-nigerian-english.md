## Summary & Key Takeaways

- Hugging Face's Open ASR Leaderboard now includes models for Egyptian and Nigerian English.
- This expands the linguistic diversity and coverage of the leaderboard.
- It highlights ongoing progress in automatic speech recognition for various dialects.
- Researchers and developers can now benchmark these specific language models.
