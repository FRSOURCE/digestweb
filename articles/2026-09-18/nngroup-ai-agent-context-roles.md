## Summary & Key Takeaways

- Identifies three key roles of context for AI agents: global, local, and ambient.
- Global context applies across multiple tasks, providing overarching knowledge.
- Local context is specific to a single task, offering immediate relevance.
- Ambient context refers to raw data streams, such as emails or notifications.
- Power users actively curate these different contexts to enhance agent performance.
- Understanding these roles is crucial for designing more effective and intelligent AI products.

## Our Commentary

This is a really insightful breakdown of context for AI agents.
