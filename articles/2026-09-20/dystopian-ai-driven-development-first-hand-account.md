## Summary & Key Takeaways

An engineer describes a new role where all development artifacts are generated by "Claude Code."
Team members are forced to ship code rapidly without understanding it.
Engineers are working 12-13 hours daily, primarily interacting with the AI.
There is a complete lack of human oversight or review of the AI-generated content.
The experience highlights potential burnout and skill degradation in AI-heavy workflows.

## Our Commentary

This quote hit me like a punch to the gut. We've talked about the promise of AI, but this is the dark side. The idea of engineers just "pressing enter" and not reading anything is genuinely unsettling. It makes me wonder about the long-term impact on skill sets, job satisfaction, and ultimately, the quality of the software being produced. This isn't just about efficiency; it's about human agency and understanding. We need to be careful not to automate away critical thinking.
