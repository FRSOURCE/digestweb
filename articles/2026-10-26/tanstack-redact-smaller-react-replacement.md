## Summary & Key Takeaways

- TanStack Redact aims to replace React and React DOM in browser applications.
- The primary goal is to reduce shipped JavaScript, leading to smaller bundle sizes.
- It offers a more lightweight alternative for frontend development.
- This could impact application performance and loading times.

## Our Commentary

TanStack is always pushing boundaries, and Redact feels like a direct challenge to the status quo. I'm genuinely curious if this can gain traction. We've seen attempts at "smaller Reacts" before, but TanStack's reputation gives this a different weight. It's a bold move, and I'm here for it.
