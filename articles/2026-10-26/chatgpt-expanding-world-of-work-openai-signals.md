## Summary & Key Takeaways

- OpenAI's Signals data tracks ChatGPT usage across 165 work activities.
- The research spans nearly 100 countries globally.
- It provides insights into how people are integrating ChatGPT into their professional lives.
- The data also shows how usage patterns evolve over time.
