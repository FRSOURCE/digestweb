## Summary & Key Takeaways

- CypressConf 2026 concluded with a focus on the theme "The Signal Is Up."
- Discussions centered on determining release readiness even when builds are green.
- Founders Brian Mann and Jennifer Shehane spoke on AI's role in the agentic SDLC.
- The conference explored how AI can help teams build more and clean less.

## Our Commentary

Conference recaps are usually a skip for us, but the "AI in the agentic SDLC" angle caught my eye. It's a buzzword-heavy phrase, sure, but the idea of AI agents handling more of the grunt work in testing is compelling. I'm always a bit wary of over-automation, but the promise of less cleaning and more building is hard to ignore.
