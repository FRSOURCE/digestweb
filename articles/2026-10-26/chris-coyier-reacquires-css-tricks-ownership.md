## Summary & Key Takeaways

- Chris Coyier has announced he is the new owner of CSS-Tricks.
- He expressed mixed feelings of being overwhelmed and nervous about the responsibility.
- This marks a significant change for the long-standing web development publication.
- Coyier had previously sold the site to DigitalOcean in 2022.
- The community reaction has been overwhelmingly positive.

## Our Commentary

This is huge news for the web dev community! CSS-Tricks has been a cornerstone for so many of us, and its recent trajectory felt a bit uncertain. Chris Coyier taking the reins again feels like a homecoming. I'm genuinely excited to see what he does with it. It's a reminder that some things are just too important to be left to corporate whims. This feels right.
