## Summary & Key Takeaways

- GPT-6.1 Terra is a new OpenAI model focused on visual reasoning and computer use.
- It aims for speed and efficiency, offering clearer, more natural writing.
- Terra is positioned as a mid-range option, cheaper than Sol but smarter than Luna.
- Pricing is $1/million input tokens and $5/million output, significantly lower than previous models.
- It excels at technical communication, vision, and computer-based tasks.
- Simon Willison provides a detailed comparison grid of OpenAI's current frontier lineup.
