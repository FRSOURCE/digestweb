## Summary & Key Takeaways

- Legora is developing an agentic operating system for legal professionals.
- It utilizes OpenAI models, including ChatGPT and Codex.

- Specific benefits include enhanced contract analysis.
- The goal is to enable lawyers to focus on more complex, high-value work.
