## Our Commentary

Benchmarking AI is such a mess right now. I appreciate Nathan Lambert digging into this. It's easy to get lost in the hype of new models without a solid way to compare them. This kind of critical analysis is exactly what we need to cut through the noise. It's a reminder that 'bigger model' doesn't always mean 'better for my specific task'.
