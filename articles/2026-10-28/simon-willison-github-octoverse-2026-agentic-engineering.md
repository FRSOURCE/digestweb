## Summary & Key Takeaways

- Simon Willison analyzes the GitHub Octoverse 2026 report.
- He highlights the stark impact of agentic engineering on GitHub metrics.
- Merged pull requests tripled to 220 million per month in less than a year.
- Commits more than tripled to 7.38 billion per month, mostly in H2 2026.
- New repository creation doubled to 30.3 million per month.
- The report's methodology used LLM classification for country and industry data.
- Interoperability repositories were identified using AI for related terms.
