## Summary & Key Takeaways

- Terk Terkowitz from Amazon engineering leadership offers advice on AI tooling.
- Engineers should document if their work doesn't benefit from current AI.
- This documentation is considered a finding, not a failing.
- Sharing these findings with peers and management is encouraged.
- Demonstrating judgment in AI adoption is a key expectation for engineers.

## Our Commentary

This is such a refreshing take from Amazon leadership. It's easy to feel pressured to adopt every new AI tool, but Terk Terkowitz is saying it's okay if it doesn't fit. In fact, it's _good_ to know why. We need more of this critical thinking, not just blind enthusiasm. It's a reminder that AI is a tool, not a mandate, and our judgment still matters.
