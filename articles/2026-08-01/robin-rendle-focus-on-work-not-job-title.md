## Summary & Key Takeaways

- The author expresses disdain for overly complex or self-important job titles in the software industry.
- He draws inspiration from AOC's perspective on ambition, which prioritizes impact over positional titles.
- Many other professional fields do not exhibit the same level of title inflation as software design.
- Focusing on the work itself, rather than vying for attention or glory, leads to more fulfilling outcomes.

## Our Commentary

I've felt this exact sentiment so many times. The sheer absurdity of some job titles in tech is genuinely baffling. It's a refreshing take to hear someone articulate that ambition should be about the _change_ you want to make, not the label you want to wear. We need more of this perspective.
