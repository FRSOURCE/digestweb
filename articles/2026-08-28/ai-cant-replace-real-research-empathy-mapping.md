## Summary & Key Takeaways

- AI is useful for organizing and synthesizing existing user research data.
- It cannot, however, create new, authentic research evidence about real user experiences.
- Empathy mapping requires understanding the "messy" and specific realities of users.
- Human researchers are essential for gathering nuanced qualitative data.
- AI tools should be seen as assistants, not replacements, for foundational research.

## Our Commentary

This is a crucial point from NNGroup. We've been saying it for a while: AI is a tool, not a sentient researcher. The "messy experience of real users" is exactly where AI falls short. It can synthesize, but it can't _feel_ or _observe_ in the human sense. I think it's important to keep reiterating this as the AI hype train continues to accelerate.
