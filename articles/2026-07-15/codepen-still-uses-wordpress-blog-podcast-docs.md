## Summary & Key Takeaways

- CodePen continues to utilize WordPress for its blog, podcast, and documentation.
- Their WordPress instance is hosted on a subdomain with a dedicated plan.
- It operates with its own distinct deployment process.
- The setup is separate from their main monorepo.

## Our Commentary

In an era where everyone seems to be chasing the latest headless CMS or static site generator, it's almost refreshing to hear a company like CodePen openly embrace WordPress. It just goes to show that sometimes the "boring" tech is the right tech for the job.
