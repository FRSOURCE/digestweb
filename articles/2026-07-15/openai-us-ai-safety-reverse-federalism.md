## Summary & Key Takeaways

- OpenAI outlines a "reverse federalism" strategy for AI governance.
- Suggests state-level laws can inform a broader national AI safety framework.
- Aims to foster safe and democratic AI development.

## Our Commentary

"Reverse federalism" is an interesting concept for AI governance. It feels like a pragmatic approach to a complex problem, allowing for experimentation at the state level before national mandates. We're going to need all hands on deck to figure out how to regulate this stuff without stifling innovation.
