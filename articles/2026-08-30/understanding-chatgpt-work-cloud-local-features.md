## Summary & Key Takeaways

- ChatGPT Work is a new, powerful product from OpenAI, available to paid subscribers.
- It exists in two forms: Work Cloud (web/mobile) and Work Local (desktop app, formerly Codex).
- Work Cloud offers advanced features like Luna and Terra models, a code execution environment, and a headless Chrome browser.
- It also includes a persistent filesystem shared across sessions and the ability to publish ChatGPT outputs.
- The distinction from regular ChatGPT Chat lies in its task-completion focus with clear outcomes.
- Simon Willison provides a clear explanation of its capabilities and when to use it.
