## Summary & Key Takeaways

- CodePen's 2.0 editor has implemented a new UI feature.
- Panels previously fixed to the left side can now be dragged.
- These dragged panels transform into "Editor Tabs."
- This provides users with significantly more freedom for their workspace layout.
- The feature aims to enhance the overall user experience and flexibility.

## Our Commentary

This is a fantastic quality-of-life improvement for CodePen users. Being able to customize your workspace layout is crucial for productivity, especially in a creative environment like CodePen.
