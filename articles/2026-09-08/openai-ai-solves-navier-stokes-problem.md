## Summary & Key Takeaways

- OpenAI claims to have found an AI-generated solution to the Navier–Stokes Millennium Prize Problem.
- The solution includes a comprehensive writeup.
- A formal proof has been provided using the Lean theorem prover.
- This represents a potential landmark achievement in both AI and mathematics/physics.
- The problem is one of seven Millennium Prize Problems, each carrying a $1 million award.
