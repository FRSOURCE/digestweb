## Summary & Key Takeaways

- Google DeepMind has launched the AlphaGenome Atlas.
- It is a predictive map of every possible DNA letter change in the human genome.
- The atlas details the molecular effects of 9 billion single-letter DNA variants.
- This represents a significant breakthrough in genomics and AI-driven biology.
- It has profound implications for understanding genetic diseases and drug discovery.
