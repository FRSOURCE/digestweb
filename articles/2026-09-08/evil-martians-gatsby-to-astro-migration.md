## Summary & Key Takeaways

- Evil Martians migrated their website from Gatsby to Astro.
- The migration was completed in a remarkably short nine-day period.
- They achieved this without rewriting any existing React components.
- The case study highlights Astro's interoperability with other frameworks.
- It demonstrates Astro's potential for fast and affordable migrations.

## Our Commentary

Nine days? Without rewriting React components? That's an impressive feat and a strong endorsement for Astro's flexibility and migration story. We've seen a lot of frameworks promise easy transitions, but this case study really puts a number on it. It makes me wonder what other complex sites could benefit from such a rapid shift.
