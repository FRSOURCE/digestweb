## Summary & Key Takeaways

- Hugging Face models are now supported on Foundry Managed Compute.
- This expands the deployment options for AI models.
- The integration aims to improve model accessibility.
- It also seeks to enhance scalability for developers.
- This provides more choices for hosting and running models.
- It reflects a broader trend of platform interoperability.

## Our Commentary

More platform integrations for Hugging Face. It's a clear strategy to embed their models everywhere. This is good for adoption, but it also fragments the ecosystem a bit. I wonder if we'll ever see a truly universal AI deployment standard, or if this proliferation of integrations is the new normal.
