## Summary & Key Takeaways

- AI inference costs are rapidly approaching zero, leading to 'virtually free intelligence.'
- This shift has profound implications for data systems.
- The article introduces the concept of 'Data Systems For Agents.'
- It also discusses 'Data Systems Of Agents' and 'Data Systems By Agents.'
- Agents are predicted to become the dominant workload for future data systems.
- The piece explores new challenges and opportunities in this era.
- It suggests a fundamental redesign of how data systems operate.
- This perspective comes from UC Berkeley's BAIR Lab.

## Our Commentary

This article is a mind-bender. "Intelligence is Free, Now What?" is a question we all need to be asking. The idea of data systems being designed _for_, _of_, and _by_ agents is a massive shift. I genuinely don't know how to feel about agents churning away at 3 am while nobody's watching, managing our data. It's exciting, terrifying, and utterly inevitable all at once. We're entering uncharted territory here, and it feels like the ground is shifting beneath our feet.
