## Summary & Key Takeaways

- Anthropic has updated the system prompts for Claude.ai and mobile apps.
- A significant new section prohibits Claude from reproducing song lyrics, poems, or copyrighted material.
- The policy extends to partial reproductions and reworded requests.
- Claude's documentation site (`platform.claude.com/docs`) is designed to be LLM-friendly.
- Users can access documentation content as Markdown by appending `.md` to URLs.

## Our Commentary

Claude's new system prompt is fascinating. The explicit ban on reproducing song lyrics and copyrighted content shows how seriously LLM providers are taking copyright issues. I also love that Anthropic's docs are LLM-friendly; that's a brilliant move for discoverability and integration. It's a subtle but important shift in how these models are governed.
