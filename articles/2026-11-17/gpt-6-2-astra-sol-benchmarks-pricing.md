## Summary & Key Takeaways

- The article analyzes OpenAI's new GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol models.
- GPT-6.2 Astra offers an 80% price reduction compared to its predecessor, with cached prices even lower.
- GPT-6.2 Sol maintains the same pricing as previous Sol versions, which were half the cost of GPT-5.6 Sol.
- Simon Willison updated his LLM tool to support the new models.
- Benchmarks were run using a complex Three.js animation prompt.
- Neither Astra nor Sol fully met the benchmark's creative requirements compared to other models.
- A detailed comparison grid and visual demonstrations of the benchmark results are provided.

## Our Commentary

Simon Willison's immediate deep-dive into the new GPT-6.2 models is exactly what we need. The pricing changes are significant, especially for Astra, making longer agentic sessions much more viable.
