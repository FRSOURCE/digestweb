## Summary & Key Takeaways

- SolidJS 2.0 is officially released, five years after its initial launch.
- A key feature is 'Async in the graph,' allowing computations to return Promises or async iterators seamlessly.
- The framework is designed to be 'Built to be written by agents,' with detailed reactive graph insights for AI tools.
- Solid 2.0 focuses on shipping primitives and framework-agnostic Vite plugins, promoting flexibility.
- The API is streamlined, removing `createResource`, `batch`, `startTransition`, `on`, `produce`, `createComputed`, and `createMutable`.
- Development builds provide stable codes, source locations, and fixes for reactive mistakes.
- The release followed 23 release candidates and extensive community contributions.
- Migration guides and updated documentation are available for existing users.

## Our Commentary

Solid 2.0 is here, and 'Async in the graph' sounds like a game-changer. We've all wrestled with async state, and the promise of it just 'handling itself' is incredibly appealing. The 'Built to be written by agents' angle is fascinating, a clear signal of where framework development is heading. I'm a little sad to see some familiar APIs go, but 'less is more' often rings true for long-term maintainability. This release feels like a significant evolution, not just an update.
