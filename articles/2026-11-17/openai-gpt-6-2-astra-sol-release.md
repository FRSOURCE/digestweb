## Summary & Key Takeaways

- OpenAI has released its new frontier models: GPT-6.2 Astra and GPT-6.2 Sol.

- GPT-6.2 Sol is positioned as an efficient, versatile model for everyday work.

- This release marks a significant advancement in OpenAI's model capabilities.
