## Summary & Key Takeaways

- Microsoft Research has launched Flint, an open-source visualization language.
- Flint is designed for the AI era, simplifying chart creation.
- It allows AI agents to generate expressive charts from concise specifications.
- The language aims to bridge the gap between simple and inspiring visualizations.

## Our Commentary

This is a smart idea. Chart specifications can be tedious, and having AI agents generate them from high-level prompts could be a huge productivity boost. The "middle path" between simple and expressive is exactly what's needed. I'm curious about the learning curve for Flint itself.
