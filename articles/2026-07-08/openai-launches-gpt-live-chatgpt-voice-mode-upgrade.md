## Summary & Key Takeaways

- OpenAI has upgraded ChatGPT's voice mode to "GPT-Live."
- GPT-Live uses a new, more impressive model for real-time conversation.
- It can delegate complex tasks requiring web search or deeper reasoning to GPT-5.5.
- The system maintains conversation flow even when delegating tasks.
- The previous voice mode was based on an older GPT-4o era model.
- A reported bug where the model would "laugh" inappropriately has been addressed.
- The upgrade significantly enhances the utility of ChatGPT's voice interface.

## Our Commentary

This is a solid step forward for conversational AI. The ability to delegate to a more powerful model in the background without breaking flow is a huge UX win. I'm glad they fixed the "interrupting laugh" bug; that sounds genuinely awkward. It's these subtle interaction improvements that really make AI feel more natural, less like a tool.
