## Summary & Key Takeaways

- The article critiques the default reliance on chat-based interfaces for AI capabilities.
- It argues that great UX requires matching AI modality to user context, intent, and cognitive load.
- Interfaces should adapt to the user, rather than forcing users into a single interaction model.
- The piece encourages exploring diverse modalities beyond conversational dialogue.

## Our Commentary

This is a much-needed perspective. We've all seen the "chat everything" trend, and it often feels forced. The point about cognitive load and matching modality to intent is spot on. I've been thinking about this a lot lately; sometimes a simple button or a structured form is far more efficient than a conversation. It's a good reminder that good design principles still apply, even with powerful new AI capabilities.
