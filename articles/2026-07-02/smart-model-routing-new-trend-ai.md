## Our Commentary

Smart model routing feels like an inevitable evolution as the LLM landscape fragments into specialized models. Why use a massive, expensive model for a simple task? This kind of intelligent orchestration is going to be crucial for cost-effectiveness and performance. I'm glad someone's surveying the field; it's a complex problem with many potential solutions.
