## Summary & Key Takeaways

- Simon Willison quotes Jack Despain Zhou on AI's impact.
- Zhou observes a "great de-individualization" due to AI.
- He notes hearing AI voices, specifically Claude, in various contexts.
- This includes YouTube, conference presentations, and professional emails.
- The concern is that AI is homogenizing human communication.

## Our Commentary

This quote really hits home. I've felt this creeping sense of sameness in AI-generated text. It's not just about accuracy; it's about the loss of unique voice, the subtle quirks that make human communication, well, human. There's something unsettling about agents churning away at 3am while nobody's watching, producing text that sounds just like everyone else's.
