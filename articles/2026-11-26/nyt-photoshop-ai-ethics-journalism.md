## Summary & Key Takeaways

- The New York Times has warned its photographers about Photoshop's AI features.
- This warning stems from the NYT's strict photojournalism policy.
- Their policy prohibits any image edits beyond cropping and balancing.
- AI features could compromise the integrity of journalistic photography.
- The goal is to present photographs exactly as they were taken.

## Our Commentary

This is a really important stance from the NYT. It highlights a critical ethical dilemma AI presents to journalism. The integrity of photojournalism relies on authenticity, and AI-driven manipulation directly threatens that. It makes me wonder how other industries will grapple with similar issues as AI tools become more pervasive. We need more clear guidelines like this.
