## Summary & Key Takeaways

- "Garden Anomaly" is a new creative experiment featured on Codrops.
- It utilizes WebGPU for high-performance graphics rendering.
- The project also employs TSL (Three.js Shading Language) for custom shaders.
- It explores complex physics simulations and real-time deformations.
- Procedural audio is integrated, reacting to the visual elements.
- The entire experience is contained within an interactive spherical space.

This is the kind of experimental web content I love to see. Pushing the boundaries with WebGPU and TSL for physics and procedural audio is genuinely exciting. It reminds me that the web can be a canvas for truly immersive and interactive experiences, not just static content.
