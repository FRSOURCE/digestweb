## Summary & Key Takeaways

- Simon Willison shares his personal journey and insights into technical blogging.
- He discusses his motivations for starting and continuing to blog.
- The interview covers surprising impacts and lessons learned from his experience.
- He highlights his most proud and most challenging blog posts.

## Our Commentary

Simon Willison's advice to "lower your standards" for blogging is something I genuinely needed to hear. It's so easy to get caught up in perfectionism and never hit publish. This is a fantastic reminder that done is better than perfect, especially when sharing knowledge. We should all take this to heart.
