## Summary & Key Takeaways

- ChatGPT's GPT-5.6 Sol model has received significant improvements.
- These enhancements focus on better accuracy and consistency in responses.
- Access to the GPT-5.6 Luna model is now expanded for all free users.
- Free users can enjoy unlimited everyday chats with GPT-5.6 Luna.
- The update aims to provide a more robust and accessible AI experience.

## Our Commentary

OpenAI continues to iterate quickly. Improving Sol's accuracy is always welcome, but the expanded free access to Luna is the real headline here. It feels like a strategic move to keep users engaged and broaden their reach, especially as competition heats up. I wonder what the "unlimited everyday chats" really means in practice.
