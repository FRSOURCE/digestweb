## Summary & Key Takeaways

- Empathy maps are a key tool in the design thinking process.
- They help UX teams visualize user attitudes and behaviors.
- The process fosters a shared understanding of end users.
- Empathy mapping also reveals gaps in existing user data.

## Our Commentary

Empathy mapping is a classic for a reason. It's foundational UX work. While not a 'new' development, it's a good reminder of the human element in all our tech endeavors. We often get caught up in the latest frameworks or AI models, but understanding the user is always the first, most important step. It's easy to forget the basics.
