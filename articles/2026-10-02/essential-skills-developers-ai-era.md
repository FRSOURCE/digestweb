## Summary & Key Takeaways

- Developers should learn to effectively direct AI agents.
- Critical review of AI-generated output is becoming a core competency.
- Maintaining strong technical judgment remains paramount in AI-assisted workflows.

## Our Commentary

This is a good reminder. We're seeing a lot of 'AI will take your job' fear, but the reality is more nuanced. It's about adapting. I think the 'critical review' part is often overlooked; it's not just about prompting, it's about knowing when the AI is confidently wrong. That's a skill.
