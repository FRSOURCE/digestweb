## Summary & Key Takeaways

- Microsoft Research explores offloaded inference for physical AI robotics.
- Moving AI inference off-robot can improve task success and efficiency.
- This approach supports more advanced physical AI workloads.
- The findings have implications for robot hardware and capabilities.

## Our Commentary

Offloaded inference for robotics is a smart move. It makes sense to leverage powerful cloud compute rather than trying to cram everything onto the robot itself. This could unlock some serious capabilities for physical AI, especially for complex tasks.
