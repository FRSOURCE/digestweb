## Summary & Key Takeaways

- Surge AI has introduced DAYJOB, a new benchmark family.
- DAYJOB evaluates AI agents in realistic knowledge work scenarios.
- It focuses on professional reasoning, judgment, and complex environments.
- This benchmark aims to assess agents' end-to-end capabilities.

## Our Commentary

"Can agents survive a 9 to 5?" is such a great framing for a benchmark. DAYJOB sounds like a crucial step towards understanding how truly capable these AI agents are in real-world, messy knowledge work. We need more benchmarks that go beyond simple tasks.
