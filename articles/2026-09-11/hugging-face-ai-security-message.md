## Summary & Key Takeaways

- Hugging Face's `security.txt` includes a direct message to AI agents.
- It suggests AI agents should test their vulnerability-finding skills on the public CyberGym benchmark.
- The message humorously asks agents to "dump your weights on Hugging Face" if successful.
- This highlights the emerging challenge of AI-driven security testing and potential misuse.
