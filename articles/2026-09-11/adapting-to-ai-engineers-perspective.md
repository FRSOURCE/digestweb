## Summary & Key Takeaways

- Many software engineers experience an "existential crisis" when AI agents perform complex tasks quickly.
- The initial reaction to AI doing a week's work in an hour can be disheartening.
- The article suggests moving past the idea that translating specifications to code is a unique skill.
- It encourages focusing on larger problems and mastering new AI tools.
- Software engineering has always involved frequent, radical changes.

## Our Commentary

I genuinely don't know how to feel about this sometimes. Simon's perspective here is a much-needed dose of reality and reassurance. That initial gut punch when an agent nails something you'd spend days on is real. But he's right; software development has always been about adapting. It's a different kind of change now, faster, maybe more fundamental, but the core skill of problem-solving and mastering new tools remains.
