## Summary & Key Takeaways

- Microsoft Research developed GigaPath-Flash and GigaTIME-Flash.
- These are efficient pathology foundation models.
- They significantly reduce computational demands.
- The models maintain strong performance despite efficiency gains.
- This innovation enables larger population-scale studies in pathology.
- It opens doors for broader exploration in medical AI.
