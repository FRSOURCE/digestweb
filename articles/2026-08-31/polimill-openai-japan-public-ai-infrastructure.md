## Summary & Key Takeaways

- Polimill is developing Japan's next-generation public AI infrastructure.
- The initiative utilizes OpenAI's GPT models and Codex.
- It aims to help municipalities search and use administrative knowledge.
- The project also focuses on accelerating development processes.
- This represents a practical application of large language models in public service.

## Our Commentary

It's always interesting to see how LLMs are being deployed in specific, real-world scenarios beyond the typical chatbot. Using them for administrative knowledge in municipalities feels like a solid use case, though I always wonder about the accuracy and verification layers needed for such critical information. Still, a good example of practical AI integration.
