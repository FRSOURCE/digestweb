## Summary & Key Takeaways

- OpenAI conducted a randomized study with over 1,000 students.
- The research investigated the interplay between ChatGPT use and critical thinking skills.
- It assessed the impact on student originality and performance in a university setting.

## Our Commentary

This is the kind of research we need more of. Moving beyond the hype to actual empirical studies on AI's impact, especially in education, is crucial. I'm always wary of studies funded by the tech itself, but the topic is too important to ignore. We need to understand the real effects, good and bad, on learning.
