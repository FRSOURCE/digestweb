## Summary & Key Takeaways

- Paul Dix highlights AI's capability to write and refine millions of lines of code.
- He references Bun's transpiler as a concrete example of AI-generated, reliable software.
- Dix argues that having an "oracle" for comparison doesn't diminish AI's achievement.
- He suggests that with proper direction and verification, AI can produce highly sophisticated software.
- The commentary explores the profound implications of AI for the future of programming.

## Our Commentary

Paul Dix's take on AI's impact, especially referencing Bun's transpiler, is thought-provoking. I agree that having a verification system doesn't make the achievement less impressive; it just changes the nature of the engineering problem. It makes me wonder how much of our future code will be "written" by AI and merely guided by us.
