## Summary & Key Takeaways

- Qwen3.8-Flash-Next is a new open-weights multimodal Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) model.
- It serves as an early preview of the architecture planned for Qwen4.
- The model is large (125B tokens) but has only 6B active parameters, boosting performance.
- Simon Willison shares his initial experiences trying out quantized versions of the model.
- This release is significant for the open-source AI community and model architecture research.
