## Summary & Key Takeaways

- Firefox has announced its intent to ship support for the JPEG XL image format.
- JPEG XL offers significant improvements over older formats like JPEG and WebP.
- It provides better compression and quality, enhancing web performance and user experience.
- This move follows the introduction of AVIF as another advanced image format in browsers.
- The adoption of JPEG XL could lead to a new standard for web imagery.

## Our Commentary

JPEG XL! Finally. We've been waiting for this one. AVIF was a good start, but JPEG XL feels like the true successor to JPEG. The potential for better compression _and_ quality is huge for web performance. I'm curious to see how quickly other browsers follow suit. This is a big win for the web platform.
