## Summary & Key Takeaways

### Key Revelations

- Sam Altman's email to OpenAI's board from October 2022 was exposed in the Musk v. Altman lawsuit.
- The email discusses OpenAI's open-source strategy.
- Altman states a desire to release a GPT-3-level model runnable locally on consumer hardware.
- The goal is to do this "soon, before Stability or someone else does."
- This move is intended to discourage others from releasing similar models.
- It also aims to make it harder for new AI efforts to secure funding.

## Our Commentary

This quote from Sam Altman is... something else. We've always suspected the 'open' in OpenAI was more of a marketing term, but to see it laid out so explicitly – a strategy to release a model _specifically_ to stifle competition and funding for open-source efforts – is jarring. It makes me question the entire narrative around democratizing AI. This isn't just business; it's a calculated move to control the ecosystem. I genuinely don't know how to feel about this, but it certainly paints a clearer picture of the competitive landscape.
