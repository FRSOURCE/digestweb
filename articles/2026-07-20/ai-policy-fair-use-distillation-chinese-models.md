## Summary & Key Takeaways

- The article explores a proposal for US AI policy regarding data collection and model distillation.
- Ben Thompson suggests explicit fair use for training data and banning TOS that forbid distillation for US companies.
- This policy aims to help US open models compete more effectively with Chinese counterparts.
- Alibaba's release of Qwen 3.8 Max as open weights is noted as a potential response to policy shifts or national directives.
- The discussion highlights the complex interplay of intellectual property, national strategy, and open-source principles in AI.
