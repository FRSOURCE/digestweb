## Summary & Key Takeaways

- Steve Yegge's "Gas Town" project ultimately failed.
- The failure is attributed to a "just two more things" development tic.
- This tic prevented the project from ever reaching a stable, usable state.
- The anecdote offers a cautionary tale about scope creep in software development.
- The quote is presented in the context of coding agents and generative AI.

## Our Commentary

Yegge's "just two more things" syndrome is a classic. It's a trap we've all fallen into, and it's even more insidious when you're building complex systems, especially with AI agents. The lesson here is timeless: sometimes you just need to ship. I appreciate Simon Willison bringing these nuggets of wisdom back into the conversation.
