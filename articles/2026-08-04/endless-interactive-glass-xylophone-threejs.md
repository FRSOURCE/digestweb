## Summary & Key Takeaways

- The project creates an endless, interactive glass xylophone experience.
- It utilizes Three.js for 3D rendering in the browser.
- Advanced techniques like instancing are used for performance.
- A fluid simulation adds dynamic visual effects.
- Glass rendering is achieved entirely through custom shaders.
