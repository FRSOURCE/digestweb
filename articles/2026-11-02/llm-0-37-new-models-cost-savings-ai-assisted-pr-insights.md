## Summary & Key Takeaways

- `llm` 0.37 introduces support for new models like `gpt-6.1-sol` and `gpt-6.1-terra`.
- A new `llm openai endpoint add` command simplifies configuring additional OpenAI-compatible models.
- The default model has been switched to `gpt-6-luna`, offering half the price of the previous default.
- Embeddings now support different binary content types, crucial for multimodal models.
- Simon Willison shares a positive experience with AI-assisted PRs, viewing them as valuable bug reports.

## Our Commentary

Simon Willison's `llm` tool is a staple for many of us experimenting with LLMs locally. The cost reduction for the default model is a welcome change, and the improved embedding support is crucial as multimodal models become more common. His take on AI-assisted PRs is also refreshing; it's good to hear a positive perspective on how AI can genuinely assist in open-source maintenance, rather than just creating noise.
