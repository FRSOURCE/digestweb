## Summary & Key Takeaways

- CodePen has implemented several upgrades to its image uploading and hosting.
- The platform migrated to the open-source file-uploading library, Uppy.
- This change resulted in faster uploads for users.
- Cost savings from the migration allowed for increased file size limits.
- Total storage limits for higher PRO plans have also been expanded.

## Our Commentary

CodePen making moves! It's always good to see platforms investing in core features like file uploads. Uppy is a solid choice, and the fact that they're passing cost savings onto users with better limits is a smart play. It's not flashy, but these kinds of DX improvements make a real difference in day-to-day work. We appreciate the little things that just _work_ better.
