## Summary & Key Takeaways

- The article addresses the morale drain in web development caused by the rise of LLMs.
- It highlights a slowdown in web education content creation across the industry.
- Piccalil emphasizes the enduring importance of human-led education and articles.
- The author was inspired by CSS-Tricks' return to independent publishing.
- It serves as a call to action for educators to continue creating high-quality content.
- The piece argues against the notion that LLMs negate the need for traditional learning resources.

## Our Commentary

'If we stop, we lose' — that title hits hard. I've felt that morale drain too, this sense that LLMs are devaluing human expertise. It's easy to get discouraged when you see comments like 'just ask an LLM.' But the truth is, LLMs are a tool, not a replacement for deep understanding and critical thinking. Geoff's return to CSS-Tricks as an independent publisher is indeed a huge shot in the arm for the community. We need more voices reminding us that quality, human-crafted education will always matter. This isn't just about teaching; it's about preserving craft and critical thought in a rapidly changing landscape.
