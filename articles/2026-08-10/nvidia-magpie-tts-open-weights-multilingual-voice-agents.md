## Summary & Key Takeaways

- NVIDIA released Magpie TTS for building voice agents.
- It features open weights, allowing for greater customization.
- Designed for low-latency, multilingual applications.
- Provides full deployment control to developers.
- Aims to enhance accessibility and performance in voice AI.

## Our Commentary

Open weights for a low-latency multilingual TTS model from NVIDIA? Yes, please. This is exactly what we need to push the boundaries of real-time voice applications. The ability to have full deployment control is also a massive win for developers who need to fine-tune for specific use cases. This feels like a solid step towards more natural and accessible AI interactions.
