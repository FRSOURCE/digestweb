## Summary & Key Takeaways

- `datasette-cluster-map` has been updated to version 0.19.
- The update ensures compatibility with Datasette 1.0a42.
- It now depends on `datasette-leaflet` for proper OpenStreetMap tile requests.
- The plugin is one of the oldest and most popular for Datasette.
- It continues to provide geospatial visualization capabilities for Datasette data.
