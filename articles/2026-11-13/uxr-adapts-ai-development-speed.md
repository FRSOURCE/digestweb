## Summary & Key Takeaways

- The article discusses the challenge of UXR adapting to fast AI development.
- It proposes a "Research-In-The-Loop" methodology.
- UXR can define key metrics for each AI release.
- Research helps teams discover insights during rapid iteration.
- This approach aims to integrate user understanding more closely with AI development.

## Our Commentary

The pace of AI development is genuinely dizzying, and I've been wondering how traditional UX processes keep up. "Research-In-The-Loop" sounds like a necessary evolution. It's a good reminder that even with AI, understanding human interaction remains paramount.
