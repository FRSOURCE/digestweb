## Summary & Key Takeaways

- Safari, powered by WebKit, is now available on Apple HomeView.
- This marks a new platform for the browser engine.
- The expansion brings web capabilities to Apple's smart home ecosystem.
- It represents a significant step for WebKit's reach beyond traditional devices.
- The announcement comes directly from the WebKit blog.

## Our Commentary

Safari on HomeView? This is a big deal. It means the web platform is extending its reach into yet another device category. I'm curious about the user experience and what kind of web content will thrive there. It's not every day a browser engine gets a whole new home.
