## Summary & Key Takeaways

- OpenAI has launched ChatGPT Wallet for its AI agents.
- Agents in ChatGPT Work, Codex, and dots can now complete purchases.
- Transactions require user approval for security.
- The wallet leverages existing card protections.
- This feature expands the practical capabilities of AI agents in real-world scenarios.

## Our Commentary

A wallet for AI agents. Wow. This feels like a huge step, and I'm not entirely sure how to process it. On one hand, it's a powerful new capability for automation. On the other, the implications of AI agents making purchases, even with user approval, are... vast.
