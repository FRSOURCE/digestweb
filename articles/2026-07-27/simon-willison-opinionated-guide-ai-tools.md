## Summary & Key Takeaways

- Simon Willison provides an evolving, opinionated guide on choosing AI tools.
- The guide notes a shift from chat-focused models to agentic systems.
- It highlights tools like ChatGPT Work and Claude Cowork for their advanced capabilities.
- Willison explains how AI companies provide computer access via modes like ChatGPT Work and Claude Cowork.
- He clarifies the distinction between mobile and desktop app functionalities for these modes.
- The article points out the unintuitive naming conventions and feature differences across platforms.
- It emphasizes that desktop apps offer more features due to direct computer access.
