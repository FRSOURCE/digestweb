## Summary & Key Takeaways

- Linus Torvalds made a strong statement regarding AI in Linux.
- He asserts that Linux is not an "anti-AI" project.
- Views AI as a useful tool, comparable to other development tools.
- Challenges dissenters to "fork it or just walk away."
- Emphasizes that AI's utility is no longer debatable.
- His stance is highly influential for the open-source community.

## Our Commentary

When Linus Torvalds puts his foot down, the open-source world listens. His declaration that 'Linux is not one of those anti-AI projects' is a massive endorsement. It's a clear signal that AI is here to stay, even in the most foundational software. I appreciate his bluntness; it cuts through a lot of the hand-wringing. 'Fork it or just walk away' – classic Linus.
