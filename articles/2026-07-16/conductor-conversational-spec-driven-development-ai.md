## Summary & Key Takeaways

- Conductor is now a portable plugin, moving beyond a Gemini CLI extension.
- It introduces conversational Spec-Driven Development (SDD) to new ecosystems.
- Developers can interact naturally with AI assistants for project specs.
- The tool dynamically manages markdown artifacts like `spec.md` and `plan.md`.
- Aims to reduce workflow friction and maintain a single source of truth.
- Ensures version-controlled project architecture across AI tools.

## Our Commentary
