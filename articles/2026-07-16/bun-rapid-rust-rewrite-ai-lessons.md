## Summary & Key Takeaways

- Examines Bun's rapid migration from Zig to Rust.
- The rewrite was completed in an astonishing 11 days.
- AI played a significant role in accelerating the process.
- The project cost approximately $165,000.
- Highlights the potential for AI to drastically shorten development timelines.
- Emphasizes the necessity of thorough testing for such rapid changes.

## Our Commentary

Bun's 11-day Rust rewrite with AI assistance is absolutely mind-boggling. $165K might sound like a lot, but for shortening a multi-year project to mere days? That's an incredible ROI. It makes me wonder what other massive refactors are now on the table for teams. This is a powerful demonstration of AI's potential to transform engineering velocity, but I'm also thinking about the quality assurance needed to pull this off.
