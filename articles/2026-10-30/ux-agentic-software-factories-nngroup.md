## Summary & Key Takeaways

- AI-native software development lifecycles risk reducing UX to mere AI context.
- The article argues UX must retain its core functions in agentic software factories.
- These functions include discovering genuine user needs.
- UX professionals should actively shape what software is built.
- Evaluation before and after launch remains a crucial UX responsibility.

## Our Commentary

This is a conversation I've been waiting for. The idea that AI might "reduce UX to AI context" is a genuine concern. We can't just let agents churn out software without a deep understanding of human needs. I think there's something unsettling about agents churning away at 3am while nobody's watching, potentially missing crucial human elements. UX needs to be more, not less, involved in this new paradigm.
