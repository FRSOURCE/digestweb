## Summary & Key Takeaways

- CodePen has launched its next-generation of feeds, including Trending and Following.
- The technology powering these feeds has been pulled in-house.
- This move aims to provide more control and potentially better performance.
- It represents an internal infrastructure shift for the platform.

## Our Commentary

It's always interesting to see platforms bring core functionality in-house. It speaks to the desire for more control and optimization. For CodePen, feeds are central to discovery, so this makes sense. I'm curious about the technical challenges they faced and how they solved them. It's a good reminder that even seemingly simple features often hide complex engineering.
