## Summary & Key Takeaways

- Evaluating Microsoft Copilot at enterprise scale requires complex test data.
- Real enterprise data contains sensitive information, making direct use impossible.
- Microsoft is using synthetic enterprise data for these evaluations.
- This approach aims to capture real-world complexity while maintaining data privacy.

## Our Commentary

This is a smart approach to a very real problem. Data privacy is a huge hurdle for enterprise AI, and synthetic data generation is becoming an increasingly vital tool. It's not just about privacy; it's about scaling evaluations without compromising sensitive information. I'm always fascinated by the clever ways researchers tackle these practical constraints.
