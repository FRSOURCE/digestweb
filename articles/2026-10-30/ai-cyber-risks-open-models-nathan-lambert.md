## Summary & Key Takeaways

- The author coined "Black October" to describe a recent surge in critical vulnerability disclosures.
- These disclosures, including PHP2Shell and GitLab, highlight the materialization of AI model cyber risks.
- The article prompts a re-evaluation of the security implications of open AI models.
- It suggests that the theoretical risks are now becoming practical concerns.

## Our Commentary

This is a conversation we absolutely need to be having. The idea of "Black October" for AI vulnerabilities is chilling. I've been optimistic about open models, but the security implications are a genuine blind spot for many, myself included. It's easy to get caught up in the capabilities and forget the potential for misuse or unintended consequences. This feels like a wake-up call.
