## Summary & Key Takeaways

- The article highlights a recent surge in security breaches, described as "a month's worth in a single day."
- It questions whether AI is a contributing factor to this increased vulnerability.
- The piece prompts a discussion on the need to fundamentally rethink software security paradigms.
- It suggests that traditional security approaches may be insufficient in the age of AI.

## Our Commentary

This is the kind of question that keeps me up at night. "A month's worth of security breaches in a single day" – that's a terrifying thought. AI is a double-edged sword; it can help with security, but it can also be weaponized. We're clearly in uncharted territory here. I genuinely don't know if our current security models can adapt fast enough. It feels like we're playing catch-up before the game has even fully started.
