## Summary & Key Takeaways

- OpenAI will now respect a shared `robots.txt` signal.
- This signal allows website operators to express their preferences for AI training data.
- The initiative aims to provide more control to content creators over how their data is used by AI.
- It represents a move towards standardized data governance for AI providers.

## Our Commentary

This is a big deal. `robots.txt` has been the de facto standard for web crawling for decades, and extending it to AI training preferences feels like a natural, if belated, evolution. It's a small step, but a crucial one for content creators to regain some agency. I'm curious to see how widely adopted this "shared signal" becomes across other AI providers. It needs to be universal to truly work.
