## Summary & Key Takeaways

- Yoav Weiss coined the phrase "WebMCP is Agentic WebPerf."
- WebMCP is a proposed protocol for AI agents to interact with web content.
- It aims to provide alternative paths for agents to consume web data.
- The concept has generated both excitement and pushback.
- Critics argue websites should just be better, or agents smarter.
- Proponents see it as a way to unlock more value with AI.
- The discussion revolves around the necessity and implications of such a protocol.

## Our Commentary

"WebMCP is Agentic WebPerf" is a provocative statement, and I'm here for the debate. The idea of a dedicated protocol for AI agents to interact with the web is fascinating, but also a bit unsettling. Are we building a separate internet for bots? I get the performance angle, but there's something about creating "alternative paths" that feels like a workaround for fundamental web design issues. It's a complex topic, and we need to watch this space closely.
