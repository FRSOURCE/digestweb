## Summary & Key Takeaways

- Third Moment Research successfully ran 10,000 AI agents concurrently.
- These agents operated for over 36 hours, demonstrating persistence.
- They utilized OpenAI models like GPT-6 Astra, GPT-6.1 Sol, Codex, and Daybreak.
- The agents are applied to cybersecurity tasks and improve with use.
- This showcases the scalability and endurance of modern AI agent systems.
