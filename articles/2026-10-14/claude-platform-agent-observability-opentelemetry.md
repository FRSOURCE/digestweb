## Summary & Key Takeaways

- Claude Platform now supports OpenTelemetry traces for its AI agents.
- This feature applies to Claude Managed Agents, the Messages API, and SDKs.
- It aims to provide better visibility into agent behavior and performance.
- Improved observability is crucial for debugging and optimizing AI agents.

## Our Commentary

Observability for AI agents is becoming critical. As these systems get more complex, understanding _why_ they do what they do is paramount. OpenTelemetry is a good standard choice here. It's a niche update, but a necessary one for serious agent development.
