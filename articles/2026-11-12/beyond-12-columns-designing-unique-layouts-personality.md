## Summary & Key Takeaways

- Many websites suffer from similar, uninspired layouts.
- Understanding a company's narrative can lead to more distinctive designs.
- The article advocates moving beyond standard 12-column grids.
- Modular and compound grids are presented as alternatives for creating unique layouts.
- These techniques help infuse personality and character into web designs.
