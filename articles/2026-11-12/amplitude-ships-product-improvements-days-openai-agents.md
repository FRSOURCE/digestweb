## Summary & Key Takeaways

- Amplitude utilizes a proactive product agent named Wave, powered by OpenAI models.
- Wave helps identify potential product improvements.
- Coding agents assist teams in shipping changes rapidly.
- The system also measures the impact of these deployed changes.
- This integrated approach allows Amplitude to ship improvements in 1-2 days.

## Our Commentary

This is a fascinating look at how AI is being integrated into the entire product lifecycle. The idea of an agent identifying what to build and then helping ship it is pretty wild. I wonder about the human oversight in such a rapid process. It's a powerful showcase, but it also makes me think about the potential for AI to introduce subtle biases or errors at scale.
