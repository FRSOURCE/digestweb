## Summary & Key Takeaways

- Simon Willison released `llm-gemini 0.37`, focusing on Google's Gemini 4 models.
- He finds the pricing for Gemini 4 Carbon, Argon, and Hydrogen confusing.
- Carbon and Argon are currently priced identically but have different performance claims.
- Hydrogen, the "cheap" model, offers only a 25% discount initially, increasing to 75% after December 1st.
- Quality differences, particularly for image generation, are stark between the models despite similar pricing.

## Our Commentary

Google's pricing strategies for their AI models are often a labyrinth, and Simon Willison's breakdown here is incredibly helpful. I mean, Carbon and Argon are the same price but one is "faster"? And Hydrogen's pricing only makes sense _after_ a future date? It's a mess. This kind of transparency and critical analysis is exactly what we need in the rapidly evolving LLM space.
