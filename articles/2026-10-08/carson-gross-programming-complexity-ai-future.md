## Summary & Key Takeaways

- Carson Gross defines programming as problem-solving and complexity control.
- He believes these skills will remain valuable despite AI advancements.
- The core value of programming as a career is expected to persist.
- AI tools are seen as augmenting, not replacing, fundamental programming skills.

## Our Commentary

I resonate with this quote from Carson Gross. It's easy to get caught up in the "AI will take our jobs" panic, but the core of programming—problem-solving and managing complexity—feels inherently human. AI can automate the mundane, but the creative, architectural thinking? That's still us. I find it reassuring, honestly.
