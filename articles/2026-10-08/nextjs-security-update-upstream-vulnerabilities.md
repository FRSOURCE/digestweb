## Summary & Key Takeaways

- Next.js is planning an out-of-band security update for October 14, 2026.
- The update will address two Critical and one High severity vulnerability.
- These vulnerabilities are located in upstream dependencies, not directly in Next.js code.
- Users should monitor the official blog for the release and apply the patch promptly.

## Our Commentary

We always appreciate transparency around security. It's good to get a heads-up like this, even if it's just an announcement of an upcoming patch. It gives us time to prepare our deployment pipelines. I just hope the fixes are straightforward and don't introduce new regressions. Security updates are a necessary evil, aren't they?
